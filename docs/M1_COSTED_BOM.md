## Population and sourcing limitations

Fox-only omits every watt-group row: PA, driver, boost, isolation power FETs, power inductor, two watt LPF cores, normal socket, heat sink and electrolytics. Its remaining count adjustments are explicit in the analysis script; source, local hardware lease/latch/POR, own low-power port, passive identity/manual calibration, pads/filter/shield and compact antenna remain.

Stock-limited VHF replaces GVA-84+ with two BFP650H6327XTSA1 devices. Its driver rail is filtered controller 3.3 V, omitting the 5 V LDO; never connect BFP650 to raw 2S or 5 V. Its <=4 V VCEO, matching, stability and output must be qualified. This is an EOL-dependent comparison with different matching/bias, not a drop-in validated alternative.

OPEN supplier part numbers, RF component values, stock conflicts, current ratings and thermal assumptions prevent an ordering-ready BOM. Custom wound coils have material allowances, with build/calibration labor separate. The proposed base has soldered RF pigtails to separate mechanically keyed bulkhead sockets. Those sockets are itemized outside the PCB target in the mandatory wiring variant, together with both halves of the detachable raw-2S feeds and the transmitter-side battery plug. The pack-side mate belongs to the external pack. Cable/crimps/strain relief are additional unquoted costs. Logic board sockets are included.

## Optional population rows

The controller USB socket/ESD, RTC hold-up capacitor and pack-current shunt, and RF-board EEPROMs remain in the CSV/catalog as optional. Essential provisioning uses service pads and a shared external fixture. A cold pack swap invalidates time and requires resynchronization before arming without backup. RF-local current/thermal hardware cutoffs, fault outputs and calibration trimmers remain essential.
