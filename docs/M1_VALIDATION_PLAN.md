# M1 qualification plan

Date: 2026-10-13. **Methods and provisional engineering limits; no tests have been performed on ARDF-TX.** Tests map to [requirements](M1_REQUIREMENTS_TRACE.md). Rule-derived constraints come from IARU-B-2025; **PROJECT TARGET** limits below are not new IARU rules or national legal authorizations. Resolve reference-power conventions/country mask before judging championship compliance.

## Equipment and records

Required: calibrated 50 Ω dummy loads rated ≥10 W continuous/≥10 W peak; calibrated HF/VHF power sensor that resolves 10 mW and AM carrier/mean; 30–40 dB RF attenuators with known loss and ≥10 W input rating; 20 dB low-power pad; directional coupler and matched terminations; oscilloscope ≥200 MHz (preferably ≥500 MHz for reliable 144 MHz carrier/envelope), RF envelope detector with linear range, suitable high-voltage/low-capacitance probes; VNA covering 3.5/144 MHz with fixture calibration; spectrum analyzer from below 3.5 MHz to ≥1 GHz, preferably 3 GHz, adequate close-in resolution/noise floor; carrier-notch/band-reject filter for dynamic range; independent calibrated frequency/time reference and counter; logic analyzer; programmable current-limited 6.0–8.4 V source/series resistance/battery simulator; current/energy logger, electronic load and pack tester; thermal chamber or controlled hot/cold fixtures/thermocouples; calibrated receiver signal generator and representative 80 m/2 m ARDF receivers, audio recorder and surveyed field distances.

An SDR can assist observation but is not the only spectrum/power/PEP standard. A low-cost VNA is useful after uncertainty/fixture characterization, not automatic calibrated ERP evidence. Coupler/pads/load cable losses must be de-embedded. Record instrument ID/calibration, bandwidth/RBW/VBW/detector/averaging, noise floor, load VSWR, voltage/current/temperature, board serial/population/firmware/calibration generation, source register plan, antenna dimensions/soil/weather/receiver settings and raw results. No calibrated spectrum facility means RF release is blocked, not waived.

Begin with low-level source/logic tests and dummy loads; radiated tests only after legal configuration and conducted gates. Qualification uses ≥3 prototype units to reveal variation, then acceptance checks all 6–10 units; antenna range evaluated at every installed location before an event. No radiation needed for controller/fault tests.

## VCTRL — independent controller and schedule

Use passive ID/load fixtures in place of RF boards. Check none/one/both/unknown modules and no LoRa. Verify power rails, current, boot pulls, reset/POR, ADC offsets, branch shorts and no signal backpower. Unknown ID/calibration/version must keep ARM low. Simulate corrupted profile, invalid time, expired run window, firmware update, brownout and battery removal/reinsertion. Check durable explicit-stop and deterministic restart policy. Configuration cannot broaden hardware limits.

Program Classic five slots and two Sprint groups using independent trace channels; test starts around each second/minute/day boundary and identifier speed extremes. Half-open slots: no late mark edge beyond boundary, including final shaped fall. Sprint pairs 1/1F,2/2F… transmit concurrently at their different fixed frequencies; per-unit 12 s/60 s, no group serialization. Confirm continuous B/S service and S absent when colocated; no beacon duty discount. Duty fractions measured from ARM, RF carrier and keyed marks separately.

**PROJECT TARGET:** local key-edge jitter ≤1 ms, absolute slot edges within ±0.5 s after 24 h without LoRa, observed overlap ≤1 s including relative clock drift. Rule allowances T3.3 are ≤5 s deviation/overlap. Start/finish clock ≤1 s T3.2 is separately applicable if this controller is used for that function. Compare temperature sweep −10…+50°C (provisional product range) to independent reference; capture oscillator faults, RTC resets, aging calibration and initial-sync uncertainty. Do not use the unit's own RTC as test clock. OTA sync during a mark must be deferred; broken LoRa leaves valid schedule intact.

## VSAFE — persistent fail-off and retune

At dummy load, record RF, ARM/KEY/LEASE, watchdog, latch and PA/source rails concurrently. Test battery insertion, slow rail ramp, ESP32 reset, unplugged/shorted ARM/KEY/LEASE, frozen firmware, SDA/SCL stuck, loss of one ground/power connection, overcurrent/reflected-power/temperature trips, RTC invalid, unknown module and reversed wiring using current-limited fixtures. Never defeat pack protection with real cells.

**PROJECT TARGET:** startup RF below measured mute floor; failed lease sets latch ≤250 ms, ordinary ARM-low to RF mute ≤1 ms after permitted shaping fall (target fall ≤8 ms); analogue fault path removes supply/input drive ≤10 ms or faster where component SOA requires. Startup has no transient arm while watchdog output initially high. Observe >10 watchdog periods after failure: Lease/watchdog output recovery must not produce recurring RF bursts. Re-enable only ARM low + explicit qualified rearm. Brownout and retained capacitor charge cannot bypass gate.

Retune while armed must be rejected. For accepted retune: measure gate isolation, discharge, update/settle/lock/readback, and next-slot enable; frequency transitions/PLL acquisition produce no observable RF at external ports. Sweep updates at highest/lowest frequency, maximum PA, low battery and hot source. Record maximum settle+gate time; provisional 100 ms guard is accepted only if it exceeds measured bound/uncertainty.

**Off-slot verification:** PROJECT TARGET ≤−70 dBm at each external RF port in narrow carrier/spur searches, with documented analyzer sensitivity, plus radiated sniff/receiver comparison to fully deenergized unit. Measure all oscillator/PA/converter/LoRa leakage paths, not PA current alone. T2.4 literally requires no RF radiation when unscheduled; finite instrument thresholds cannot prove mathematical zero or create an exemption. Investigate any repeatable detectable DUT-correlated signal; record residual detection limit.

## V80P — 80 m source, PA, filtering and power ranges

Independently supply/command board from bench host. Start without PA, confirm legal frequency registers/readback and gate behavior, then current-limited PA into 50 Ω. Measure at band ends and every competition channel, full 6.0–8.4 V pack range and hot/cold. Sweep reduced/maximum settings Classic 1–5 W and Sprint 0.3–1 W, record mark power/current/filter loss/thermal limits. Set actual upper calibration below nominal ceiling enough that uncertainty interval stays inside requirement; endpoint labels must not force over-limit output.

For sine RF in 50 Ω, `P=Vpp²/(8R)`; expected Vpp: 5 W≈44.72 V, 1 W=20 V, 10 mW=2 V. Use post-LPF point, not class-E drain waveform; verify waveform/harmonics before relying on this equation. Power-meter uncertainty target ≤5% for watts, ≤0.5 dB at 10 mW; record actual calibrated uncertainty.

Measure CW 10–90% rise/fall, overshoot and time-aligned spectrum across WPM extremes and last-symbol truncation. **PROJECT TARGET** smooth 3–8 ms normal rise/fall, overshoot ≤10%, no repeatable audible keying clicks on adjacent receiver at ±20 kHz. These are proposed limits; spectrum/legal mask and receiver evaluation take precedence. Emergency off may interrupt shaping. Verify class-E drain peak has ≥20% margin to rated breakdown under normal specified load; if BS170/15 V lacks margin, change PA/rail rather than waive. Mismatch all reflection phases at 3:1 VSWR, then controlled open/short with protection response; no destructive continuous mismatch assumed permissible.

## V80A — longer Classic/Sprint wire and matching

Dummy-load success precedes radiation. Measure match S11 with calibrated antenna fixture, input/reactance, tuner insertion/loss estimate, antenna current and capacitor/coil voltage under low and full power. Log radiator length/height, radial count/length/angle, soil conductivity proxy/moisture, support proximity and enclosure. **PROJECT TARGET** tuned source-side VSWR≤2:1 across approved channels; limit depends on PA characterization and is not IARU antenna impedance. Check capacitor/arcing/Q/coil heating and safe operation with tuning incorrect.

Use identical validated installation recipe across competition field transmitters (T2.2) and do not change antennas during competition (27.2). Sprint needs symmetric grounding (S6.6); an illustrative single 6 m return wire cannot qualify solely by length. Map horizontal received levels at ≥8 azimuths and multiple distances; PROJECT TARGET ripple ≤3 dB after terrain uncertainty where practicable, plus referee approval of omnidirectional pattern. Confirm audibility from start with average-sensitivity receivers and organizer monitoring/recording (27.3). Repeat dry/wet and two representative sites. Match/pattern criteria may require a different radial recipe.

## V80F — compact Foxoring conducted power and coupling

Bypass installed cartridge **only for calibration** and connect defined post-LPF reference output to a calibrated 50 Ω load. Measure nominal **10 mW mark power**, spectrum and supply/temperature/frequency variation. Proposed engineering repeatability 10 mW±10% is **not** a rulebook tolerance; include uncertainty and secure referee agreement for final reference convention/tolerance. A broadband power meter averaging Morse duty cannot establish mark power. Confirm other paths disabled.

Then attach the **separate** ~30 cm radiator, compact counterpoise and cartridge; de-embed fixture capacitance. Record its base complex impedance, loading/tap/coupling component values, Q/SRF, resonant sweep/bandwidth, RF voltage/current, loss sensitivity and expected component stress. Measure with actual protected 1P pack/enclosure; hands, wet leaves/soil and alternate 2P–4P battery geometry can change return coupling. Establish a range-compatible repeatable coupling recipe at fixed reference power. Do not demand near-perfect 50 Ω match if deliberate coupling/loss gives correct field behavior; define the chosen source loading and accepted mismatch explicitly.

Inject wrong software power/path, normal PA into dummy load while compact antenna attached, and compact-ID open/short/wrong code. Verify hardware watt inhibit and permanent bounded branch. **PROJECT TARGET:** training/compact source cannot exceed validated low-branch ceiling under single commanded control faults; if nominal setting tolerance is ±10%, characterize guard limit before adopting it. No ordinary routing switch connects PA to cartridge. Measure normal-PA capacitive/inductive feedthrough into compact port/network and RF stress at its terminals. Cable/mating swaps and possible deliberate adapters are documented limits of mechanical keying; normal antenna port labels alone are insufficient. Include connector/protection loading in final tune.

## VFIELD — reproducible Foxoring audibility

After legal/conducted gates, select at least three field environments: open dry grass, dry woodland, wet woodland. Survey real antenna location and mapped nominal point; actual/mapped points may differ. Use ≥8 azimuths at **30 m and 250 m** and the mapped point. Maintain defined receiver antenna height/orientation and a repeatable slow orientation sweep; record background noise before/after and transmitter-off controls. Fix receiver/BFO/filter/AGC/audio gain and headphones, plus document a representative average-sensitivity receiver and a more sensitive one. Define an audio scoring method before test: e.g. three independent listeners must reliably identify ≥4 of 5 recorded code sequences at required-audible points; at 250 m blinded on/off recordings must not yield reproducible code recognition. Audio SNR is recorded but does not replace human audibility/referee judgment.

**RULE:** clearly audible at nominal mapped position and 30 m, not audible at 250 m throughout competition. 250 m checks must include favorable propagation directions/receiver orientation, not only weakest direction. The rule does not prescribe receiver bandwidth/gain/noise threshold; agree the reproducible receiver/listener protocol with organiser. Repeat with battery full/near cutoff, frequency extremes, person standing near/leaving unit, wet cartridge/radials, vegetation and at least three separate installations. Log full recipe, uncertainty and raw recordings. If dry site fails 30 m or wet site passes 250 m, revise coupling/installation and repeat; a nominal 10 mW result cannot rescue the failed field gate. Recheck each event site and monitor during competition. Finish beacon uses V80A, normal watts and start-audibility, not these restricted-range criteria.

## V2AM — true A2A, PEP, ERP and VHF source

Verify source alone with all approved channel registers before PA. Competition profile enforces 144.500–144.900 MHz independently of local-band capability. Check frequency stability **better than 50 ppm**, absolute calibration and thermal/battery/supply-noise behavior; project target ≤10 ppm total if achievable. Nearby continuously transmitting channels must be **more than 200 kHz** apart; a 200 kHz difference is a fail. Flexible 144–146 MHz testing is into loads until local authorization/channel compatibility confirmed.

Use RF sample after normal LPF and a calibrated linear envelope detector/scope. For every Morse mark measure `m=(Vmax−Vmin)/(Vmax+Vmin)`; require **0.70≤m≤0.80 including uncertainty**, target nominal 0.75. Inspect for flattening, trough leakage, parasitic FM/AM-to-PM and startup transients at all RF levels, frequencies, temperatures and pack voltages. **PROJECT TARGET** envelope-tone THD≤5%, tone ~800 Hz with stable amplitude, smooth AF mark edges; tighten/adjust by spectral acceptance. A square envelope or constant-amplitude carrier with FM sidetone fails. Demonstrate continuous carrier + keyed AF or keyed carrier + AF, as chosen in ADR-0005.

Measure unmodulated carrier Pc, mark mean and RF-cycle PEP separately, compare with `Pc(1+m)²`, and verify ≥1 dB clean envelope capability above calculated maximum. A slow average wattmeter cannot measure PEP. Test DC safe operating area, modulator dissipation, boost droop and current peak with modulation. If test fixture measures 1 W connector power, do not relabel as 1 W ERP.

ERP: characterize installed horizontal antenna's maximum horizontal gain/pattern relative to half-wave dipole, measured cable/balun loss and mounting at **2–3 m**. Conducted substitution range measurement with a calibrated reference dipole/antenna is preferred; account for ground/multipath and uncertainty. Record carrier/mean/PEP ERP and EIRP separately; EIRP=ERP×1.64 for consistent reference. Require agreed competition ERP convention and 0.25–1 W range including uncertainty; horizontal omnidirectionality per T2.5. Ordinary VNA return loss does not establish gain/ERP. Re-run power sizing if net gain below the model's −1 dB boundary.

## V2TRAIN — isolated low-power path

Characterize separate output into 50 Ω across proposed 1 µW–1 mW carrier range with sufficient instrument dynamic range; confirm A2A envelope/PEP and own filter. Operate normal PA into dummy load and search training port for feedthrough; test mode bits/control shorts, loss of ID and simultaneous conflicting requests. No commanded fault may route watt RF to training connector; measured leakage must stay within the branch's approved power ceiling. Training field test: football-field-sized site, fixed receiver/settings, ≥8 headings and near/far positions, adjust permanent attenuation/antenna recipe to agreed reach. No rule or µW label supplies blanket local authorization.

## VSPEC — unwanted emissions and silence

Measure every external RF path at min/max level and all permitted source configurations, including Si5351 alternate and PLL acquisition. Spectrum plan: narrow scans inside channel around tone/keying, close offsets 1 kHz–2 MHz, wide spans through ≥10th HF harmonic and ≥1 GHz VHF (preferably 3 GHz), converter/reference/ESP32/LoRa clocks. Compare with national applicable test mask; IARU defers unwanted-product limits to national law. Until a country mask is resolved, **PROJECT TARGET** HF undesired discrete products ≤−50 dBc; VHF ≤−60 dBc below 1 GHz and ≤−50 dBc above, measured against carrier, excluding intentional in-channel AM sidebands. Low-power branches need an agreed absolute limit; no arbitrary relative target becomes legal permission. Review occupied-bandwidth limits separately.

Record analyzer linearity by adding 10 dB input pad; apparent products moving nonlinearly may be instrument overload. De-embed pad/filter response; use carrier rejection if needed to see close-in products. Output LPF does not remove synthesis spurs within passband. Broad PLL noise can desensitize simultaneous receivers even if individual lines look small; evaluate adjacent channel noise/audibility and T4.1. Source/cable leakage must be measured in off slots as VSAFE.

## Additional cost-rework qualification

Direct RF control and reduced populations undergo the same tests. Verify both serial buses with absent/shorted boards, manual type/serial/calibration-record binding and optional EEPROM corruption. Without RTC backup, a cold pack swap must invalidate time and prevent ARM until synchronization. Test all PWM power/envelope levels for ripple, clipping, CW edges and modulation error; no DAC does not waive waveform acceptance.

For HC123/POR/latch, sweep component tolerance, supply ramps and temperature; test heartbeat stuck high/low and a frozen task with timer peripherals still running. The lease represents fresh application progress. After timeout/fault, recurring heartbeat cannot rearm without a fresh qualified ARM edge. Source power-off must block SCL/SDA phantom power; source/PA shutdown and bleeder discharge remain independent of software.

Compare HF single-IRF610 and class-E alternatives at the same 5 W connector setting and all channels. For VHF, determine driver power from measured PA gain; verify 4.079 W PEP and at least 5.14 W clean capability under the stated ERP/gain convention. Published 500 MHz transistor data or fT/compression headlines do not establish a 144 MHz AM pass. Candidate low-port coupling budgets (47 dB HF /77 dB lowest training setting) require calibrated leakage and near-field receiver checks; recost shielding if necessary.

**Cost acceptance:** obtain complete live 6/10/20 same-MPN baskets, count packaging leftovers/spares and distinguish taxes, wiring, manufacturing and calibration labor. Every required protective/filter/matching item needs a quote or explicit estimate. Compare individual board and combined targets. Record EOL availability separately from future repeatability and require explicit finite-batch policy before relying on such parts. No ordering occurs under this plan.

## VPWR, VTHERM, VLORA and fleet repeatability

**VPWR:** log total pack V/I/Wh in each profile and absent/standby/duty/continuous-RX LoRa state. Measure startup, retune, PA peak envelope, loaded cutoff and RF-board standby separately. Exercise every 1P–4P pack with safe current-limited fixtures first, then protected cells; test ESR, cold capacity, imbalance/BMS cutoffs and reconnection. Validate ≥24 h with ≥20% capacity aging allowance or equivalent test/derating model, specified RF setting maintained to end; no “still running” pass after RF droops below range. Compare measured mean power to pack ceilings in [budget](M1_POWER_BUDGET.md). Continuous beacon tests have their own shorter feasible runtime; never inherit field duty. Firmware must report inadequate pack/runtime rather than promise 24 h.

**VTHERM:** sealed intended enclosure at ambient extremes, maximum scheduled profiles plus continuous beacon stress. Log PA/modulator/boost/coil/connector/cell temperatures, power/modulation drift and thermal trips. PROJECT TARGET ≥20°C junction margin to manufacturer limit using measured case/thermal model; accessible surfaces and cells must meet selected product/pack limits. Safe trips do not prove uninterrupted contest endurance. Rework 15 V BS170 if breakdown/thermal margin fails.

**VLORA:** absent, floating, stuck BUSY/DIO, wrong/failed module, timeout and interrupted SPI while schedules continue. No detection TX. With module present, verify authenticated commands/replay rejection, persistent stop and autonomous holdover. Into dummy loads and then legally in representative antennas, run 80 m/2 m RX/TX and LoRa listening/bursts concurrently; include VHF channels whose sixth harmonic approaches selected LoRa channel. PROJECT TARGET no ARM faults/timing miss, power shift ≤5%, AM remains 70–80%, LoRa sensitivity degradation ≤3 dB in controlled blocking test, and no audible interference in ARDF receivers. Separate conducted desense, radiated coupling and power-rail effects. Operational scheduling of LoRa can reduce coupling but does not waive hardware immunity tests.

**VFLEET:** record build/winding/firmware/antenna versions and calibration per serial; verify RF/power/envelope/off/startup/fault/timing on every delivered unit. Repeated connector insertion, wet/dry installation and substitute component lots must not silently alter match/field range. Archive source/right-to-reuse decisions. M2 may authorize prototypes only after user decisions; these procedures cannot be checked “passed” without measurements.
