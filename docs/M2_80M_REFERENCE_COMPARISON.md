# Proven 80 m circuits: source and schematic comparison

Date: **2026-10-13**. **Research completed; derived hardware NOT VALIDATED.** This review preserves M1, M2 preparation and the Foxoring audit. Recommendations are in [the decision](M2_80M_DECISION.md); [topology](M2_80M_RF_TOPOLOGY.md), [costs](M2_80M_COST_COMPARISON.md), [power](M2_80M_POWER_COMPARISON.md) and [gates](M2_80M_VALIDATION_GATES.md) distinguish facts from proposed adaptations.

Evidence classes: **SCHEMATIC/BOM** means inspected original files, **AUTHOR MEASUREMENT** means the publisher describes a test, **AUTHOR CLAIM/BUILT** means specifications or photographed construction, **CALCULATION/ESTIMATE** is ours, and **NOT RUN** means our hardware evidence is absent. No independent calibrated measurement of the proposed ARDF-TX derivative was found or performed. Original designs being built does not qualify a changed circuit.

These numbers describe different reference planes, supplies and loads. Neither field audibility nor antenna current is ERP, PEP or 50Ω connector power. An A1A mark has equal unmodulated carrier power and PEP; duty-averaged meter readings differ. None of these CW references substitutes for the separate 2 m A2A architecture.

## Physical layout and reproducibility findings

Original FlexFox80 PCB has **four copper layers**, edge bounding box 111.35×80.15mm; SignalSlinger also has **four layers**, 94.22×59.155mm. Dimensions were read with KiCad's board-edge API; original copper/silkscreen PDFs were visually inspected. These are whole reference products, not the minimum extracted module size. Do not assign a two-layer fabrication price to their native boards.

Both place LM5134, final and transformer near the RF filter, use hand-wound toroids and a metal-tab heat path. Reuse the short high-current gate/drain loop and separated filter nodes as constraints, not their complete board shapes, connectors or layer stack. Toroid winding direction, tap identity, core material, distributed capacitance and supply-loop return are reproducibility-critical. Substituting a generic “2.7µH” inductor or yellow/unknown core is not established equivalence. Exact native capacitor voltage/footprint variants must be checked: Flex BOM versions disagree on 91p package. A cheaper PCB layer count remains a future measured/layout decision.
