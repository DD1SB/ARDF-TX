## Peaks and acceptance

The VHF 35% efficiency envelope crest plus 1 W peak base/helper provision, LoRa and 25% transient margin gives about **4.5 A at 6 V**, a calculation. Provision **≥5 A single-band** protected power/connector/BMS paths. Simultaneous dual-TX needs its own waveform/inrush/current qualification and≥7 A initial path provision; poor-efficiency cases can require more. No fuse/current-limit rating is released. Measure converter switch current separately from pack/PA currents and test all 1P–4P geometries/BMS/ESR/cold cutoff.

Continuous full-power HF/VHF and conservatively budgeted1W Sprint beacons **do not reach 24 h within4P**. Their event duration or power arrangement is a separate user input; do not apply20% duty or silently reduce required RF settings. A unit remaining powered after RF droops below specification is a fail. Real discharge and temperature tests must verify the selected profile, optional-LoRa state and end-of-run RF level.
