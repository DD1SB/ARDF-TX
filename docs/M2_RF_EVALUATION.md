# Minimum RF evaluation hardware and go/no-go gates

Date: **2026-10-13**. **Evaluation plan; no hardware built, purchased or measured.** M1 architecture is conditionally accepted. Circuit development/evaluation preparation proceeds; complete product layout, procurement and full-set manufacture remain gated. This plan supplements [M1 qualification](M1_VALIDATION_PLAN.md), the [VHF review](M2_VHF_RF_REVIEW.md) and [HF proposals](M2_HF_CIRCUIT_PROPOSALS.md).

## Minimum reusable hardware

Use **one initial set of reusable evaluation fixtures**, then repeat critical RF measurements on at least three independently assembled source/driver/PA samples before committing the complete module layout. They are laboratory fixtures, not a fourth product-board architecture or a transmitter fleet. Do not build six/twenty complete units to discover a basic drive or modulation failure.

| Fixture | Minimum population / function | Isolation and test access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E0 power/control/safety fixture | Current-limited 2S-equivalent supply; reviewed 20 V-rated boost candidate for 13.5 V VHF/15–16 V HF, never TPS61088 above 12.6 V; 3.3 V logic; host I2C/control interface; application-progress lease input, ARM, mode, MOD and LEVEL; POR/latch/current/temperature inputs and independently gated source/PA rails | Expose rails, lease, latch, nFAULT, shunt and discharge points. A bench stimulus is not validated controller firmware. Manual emergency inhibit independent of host. |
| E1 local source + MMIC coupon | Local Si5351/reference and quiet supply; fundamental filter/pad; GVA-84+ with specified bias network and 50 Ω input/output | Source-only bypass; standalone driver generator input; taps before/after pad; heat/rail monitoring. Compare PHA only if margin/gain warrants it. |
| E2 VHF PA coupon | One formed-lead PD55008 with mechanically appropriate grounding/heat removal, variable gate bias, replaceable input/output match, LPF and supply/drive cutoff | Feed calibrated RF generator directly first; independent variable drain supply before AM integration. Document all coupling losses. Short RF connections, shielding and calibrated reference planes are required. |
| E3 AF modulator/load coupon | D45H11/BD139-class helper, feedback amplifier/compensation, current/overvoltage limits, upstream isolation, heat sink and small output capacitance | External keyed sine input; 100 nF default vs controlled capacitance stress. Start with a ≥25 W load near 14.2 Ω plus optional 150 mA current sink to emulate the calculation; measure actual resistance/current. Then connect E2. |
| E4 HF watt + bounded-low-path coupon | Local source, industrial gate driver, AC gate protection, IRF610, boost/isolation/shaping, interchangeable LPF; independently supplied low buffer/pad/filter and interlock | Populate/test low path first with watt stage absent. Watt stage only into rated load; scope gate/drain/current. Both RF paths retain separate physical outputs. |
| E5 two passive HF antenna fixtures | Longer-wire match and defined symmetric return; separate compact loading/coupling cartridge, ~30 cm radiator and compact counterpoise | Input/reference adapter, identity contact, actual enclosure/pack geometry, repeatable tuning/field records. No common watt-to-compact RF switch. |

Solderless plug-in breadboards are unsuitable for judging 144 MHz gain/stability or powered RF. A PA evaluation coupon may require a purpose-made small RF carrier, but its layout is not supplied or ordered here. Its reference-plane, grounding, heat and replaceable match are part of the subsequent coupon design review. Do not copy manufacturer 500 MHz layouts and assume they qualify 144 MHz.

Candidate **one-off material allowance**, excluding instruments and labor: E0 €8–15, E1 €8–15, E2 €20–35, E3 €8–15, E4 €20–35, E5 €20–35. Total approximately **€84–150 estimated** before shared cables/loads and shipping. These are functional allowances, not supplier quotes or authorization to order; avoid double-buying a common source/safety fixture. Commercial evaluation modules are alternatives only after obtaining prices and confirming the needed test access. Replication/spares add cost after first-stage gates pass.

## Instruments that make the gates measurable

| Measurement | Required capability | Minimum method / uncertainty record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Driver power/gain/compression | Calibrated 144–146 MHz generator, known step attenuation, HF/VHF sensor and 50 Ω loads | Sweep input/output; establish P1dB and ≤0.2 dB compression at reserved drive. De-embed pads/filter/coupler losses and evaluate actual PA loading. |
| Carrier, mean and PEP / AM | ≥200 MHz oscilloscope, preferably ≥500 MHz; calibrated linear envelope detector/RF sampler; ≥10 W load and rated sampling attenuator | Measure Pc, mark mean, Vmax/Vmin and RF-cycle PEP separately; slow average meter cannot substitute for envelope/PEP. Target power uncertainty ≤5%, low-power uncertainty ≤0.5 dB. |
| Synthesis and spurs | Spectrum analyzer through ≥1 GHz, preferably 3 GHz, adequate close-in RBW/noise floor; rated input pads and optional carrier rejection | Source-only and full-chain narrow/wide scans; overload check by adding a known pad. SDR is useful supplementary evidence. |
| Matching/stability | Calibrated VNA, DC blocks/bias fixture, dummy loads and controlled mismatch fixtures | Cold passive network loss/impedance first; powered large-signal matching and broadband parasitic searches separately. Small-signal S parameters do not prove power gain. |
| Modulator/thermal/SOA | AF generator, adjustable electronic load/resistors, current probe/shunt and energy logger; thermocouples; controlled ambient/hot fixtures | Log voltage/current throughout envelope, case temperatures and startup/fault pulses. Exact-vendor SOA/thermal evidence plus measured trajectories. |
| Battery power and timing | Current-limited 6.0–8.4 V source with series resistance, independent timing/frequency reference and logic analyzer | Record active/idle/retune/LoRa loads and 24 h input Wh. Real protected-pack discharge/cold/ESR remains a later measured gate. |
| Compact field reach | Representative calibrated ARDF receiver(s), audio recording, surveyed 30/250 m paths and transmitter-off controls | Blind audible-code protocol, several azimuths/environments and actual pack/antenna installation. Bench dummy-load power does not substitute. |

Borrowing/renting a calibrated lab may be cheaper than buying suitable instruments. Its availability and price are **open**, not a free assumed resource. Instrument capital is separate from transmitter material cost. No measurement capability means its gate remains open.

## Gate sequence and explicit acceptance

Every numeric engineering limit below is a **PROJECT TARGET** unless explicitly traced to IARU in [requirements](M1_REQUIREMENTS_TRACE.md). Resolve national emissions mask and referee power conventions before a compliance/release judgment. Each gate is currently **NOT RUN / NO-GO for release**. A paper or ideal-simulation result permits continued investigation, not a hardware pass.

| Gate | Go criterion | No-go / required response |
| --- | --- | --- |

No backpower through buses; valid retune shows source/PA mute throughout. Emergency ARM/fault mute bypasses CW shaping, with ARM-low target ≤1 ms; analog trip ≤10 ms or faster as required by qualified SOA; lease loss ≤250 ms. | Any startup burst, rearming on heartbeat alone, or PA/source remaining powered through a bypass: repair before powered RF escalation. |

| G2 VHF driver | With worst measured P1dB, actual PA gain/losses and clean reserve, **≥3 dB driver backoff**, measured compression≤0.2 dB and stable operation across frequency, rail, load, temperature and three samples. | GVA insufficient: compare stronger current MMIC/source drive or PA matching; do not fit BFP650 or reduce required ERP/PEP. |

The RF power capability tests and calibrated competition settings are separate: demonstrate required headroom into loads, then bound operational settings so their uncertainty interval stays within the agreed rule/legal range. No capability loss is hidden by a lower label. A subsequent complete module layout is **go only after its relevant source, drive, envelope, safety, thermal and spectrum gates have measured evidence** and open layout-dependent limits have an explicit final qualification plan. Field/pack/integration gates remain necessary for product/competition release even where their last checks need the final enclosure/controller.

## Measurements simulation cannot replace

1. **Actual source spurs and silence** across divider settings, rails and nearby clocks/LoRa.
2. **Loaded 144 MHz driver compression, PA gain and AM envelope/PEP**, including voltage-dependent impedance, trough leakage and parasitic oscillation.
3. **Modulator and helper temperature/current/SOA trajectories**, DC/no-tone operation, capacitive-load stability and fault interruption.
4. **Powered HF efficiency, drain/gate stress, CW clicks and real filter loss/harmonics**, including hand-wound variation and mismatch.
5. **Low-port RF feedthrough and transient ceilings**, actual interlock/startup/brownout/retune behavior.
6. **Compact 30/250 m audibility, antenna gain/pattern and installation sensitivity** with real ground, counterpoise, case and pack geometry.
7. **Real pack energy/peak current/cold cutoff, autonomous timing and optional-LoRa immunity** on representative hardware.

The ideal LPF simulations have actually run; they verify only the modeled passive transfer. The envelope integration and S-parameter arithmetic are **calculations**, not measured or nonlinear transistor-simulation results. No go mark has been assigned to hardware.
