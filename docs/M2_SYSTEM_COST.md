## Endurance and release boundary

All pack sizes are electrically compatible candidates; their inclusion in a cost table does not mean they meet every 24 h profile. Conservative VHF preparation uses **4P (about 27.1 h modeled)**; a low-bias measured chain might qualify 3P, while poor efficiency fails even 4P. HF Classic uses 4P, Sprint 2P, Foxoring 1P in current planning; Sprint 1P requires efficiency/current closure. Continuous full-power beacons and simultaneous dual-band 24 h still fail within 4P. See [VHF review](M2_VHF_RF_REVIEW.md) and [revised full profile budget](M2_POWER_BUDGET.md); the M1 profile assumptions are retained as history.

**No full-set manufacture or order.** Replace allowances with complete comparable baskets only after circuit and RF gates close. A decision to use a safer pass device or better filter takes precedence over the old component targets; its full material, heat and battery consequences remain visible.
