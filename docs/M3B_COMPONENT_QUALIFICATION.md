## Wound parts and assembly checks

**T401** is the original **FT-50A-43**, not the thinner FT50-43 substituted by name. Core dimensions are nominal12.7 mm OD/7.92 mm ID/6.35 mm high. Wind two **9-turn AWG24 enamel wires**, about24 cm each as a starting length, bifilar and same sense. Astart1/dot, Afinish2, Bstart3/dot, Bfinish4;2+3 join the drain. One pass through the hole is one turn. Confirm series-aiding phasing with a low-energy generator before fitting the PA. Starting AL480 nH implies38.9 µH per winding; this is a calculation, not a measured transformer. The formed-lead fixture now has inline1/2/3/4 at5.08 mm pitch,2/3 adjacent,1 mm drills. The magnetic/electrical reference is unchanged; lead geometry is new and needs dry-fit and RF testing.

**L410/L411** retain MicrometalsT60-2, **20 turns AWG24**, approximately46 cm starting wire length. Nominal AL6.5 nH gives2.60 µH; original labeled2.7 µH is retained pending measurement/trimming to the original filter response. Core nominal15.24/8.53/5.94 mm; use max15.75/min8.03/max6.45 envelope plus winding. L410 flat, L411 upright and orthogonal. Do not substitute unidentified powdered-iron material or explain away a wrong measured inductance. Local models include winding allowance and do not certify winding parasitics.

**L510/L511** retain Bourns **78F2R7J-RC**2.7 µH ±5%, original axial landing/forming and current limits; these are not the watt filter toroids. Core/wire suppliers and estimate status are retained in the BOM. No unknown marketplace “equivalent” has been qualified.

## Availability and procurement blockers

Manufacturer pin/package documentation was reviewed or retained from R1; **no blanket statement of all parts in stock is justified**. Existing observations retain date/localization limitations. LM5134B exact EU cut-tape supply, HC174 basket, custom-wound core availability, capacitor ESR evidence, pulse-resistor qualification and harness/contact exact codes must be settled before component procurement. MOQ1 cut-tape assumptions are explicit in the CSV; no reel-only purchase or nonexistent supplier number is invented. Exact incoming component inspection and a1:1 printed-footprint fit are Stage0 gates.

The board has no purchased test connectors beyond the original module connectors. Native standard-body models and eight own dimensional envelopes are identified separately. No heatsink, sensor bond or exact connector mating clearance is qualified merely by a3D picture. These uncertainties do not prevent a **bare evaluation board** from being independently reviewed; they block unrestricted hardware commissioning and product release.
