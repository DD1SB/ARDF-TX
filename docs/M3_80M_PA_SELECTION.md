# M3A — selected 80 m power stage

Date: **2026-10-14**. Electrical design review, **not hardware validation**. The current mission authorizes the 80 m schematic at approximately 3 W full / 1.2 W extended on 4S, and a separate 2S-compatible Fox population. It does not accept all of proposed ADR-0010/0011, authorize layout or alter the 2 m **1 W carrier ERP, 70–80% sinusoidal A2A** target or its PEP gates.

Select **SignalSlinger/FlexFox80's local Si5351 → LM5134B → one IRF610 → bifilar autotransformer → original elliptic filter**, supplied by **one TPS54302 buck**. Use the QCX/W6JL drain-envelope integrator principle with a currently manufactured **ST BD140** pass transistor. This is the implementation in [editable KiCad](../hardware/80m/RF80.kicad_sch), [review PDF](../hardware/80m/review/RF80.pdf) and [circuit review](M3_80M_SCHEMATIC_REVIEW.md). No competing complete product schematic is created.

All other module allowances are deliberately retained in the substitution rather than credited as free safety deletions. The estimated €0.19 difference at ten is below the unquoted-material uncertainty. HG0HK remains uncostable without its circuit.

Thus IRF610 is preferred for documented programmable reuse, wider drain-voltage margin and simpler matching/driver integration, **not because a measured or price-closed global cost minimum was proved**. If driver stock or RF testing requires revisiting QCX, use the same external source/control fixture, current-limited supplies, calibrated adapters/load and instruments described in the [test plan](M3_80M_TEST_PLAN.md). Only the PA/driver/matching assembly changes; no second full controller/transmitter is needed. This is a shared evaluation contract, not another product layout.

## Electrical margins driving the choice

IRF610 is rated200 V drain and±20 V gate; that is a component limit, not measured RF survival. Our prototype limits are **≤100 V repetitive drain peak, ≤12 V gate peak, TJ≤110°C**, including mismatch tests. Observe true low-capacitance drain waveforms; neither the 50 Ω wattmeter nor DC drain rating establishes them. Gate power from the datasheet's typical8.2 nC charge at10 V gives `Qg × Vdrive × f ≈0.232 W` at7.96 V/3.55 MHz. Charge at our exact rail/load differs; this is a driver-loss estimate, not measured current. [Vishay IRF610](https://www.vishay.com/docs/91023/irf610.pdf), [TI LM5134](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm5134.pdf).

The original QCX MPS751 has a625 mW free-air DC limit at25°C and no adequate published hot repetitive pulse SOA for our altered RC/current trajectory. Central's **E–B–C** pinout also differs from many substitute transistors. ST BD140 retains one PNP and the same integrator connections, improves package/thermal/SOA evidence, and costs **€0.264 at ten versus €0.72** for the selected MPS751. Its **E–C–B** TO-126 pinout is implemented explicitly. This substitution is justified by thermal evidence as well as cost; it is **not authorization to put BD140 back in the VHF AM design**. [Central MPS751](https://my.centralsemi.com/datasheets/MPS650_MPS750_SERIES.PDF), [ST BD140](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/bd140.pdf).
