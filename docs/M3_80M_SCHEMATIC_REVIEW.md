## Power and normal PA

J301 supplies an **externally protected** pack; F3011.5 A candidate fuse and SS34 reverse barrier are shared. Only NORMAL population contains the buck, output switch, shunt/sensor, driver, final, shaper, transformer, normal filter and terminal. 4S input12.0/14.4/16.8 V is the normal test envelope. Fox-only uses protected2S1P and the controller's separately regulated3.3 V; it still connects J301 for safe pack sensing. No raw battery pin feeds an ESP32/Si5351/HC00 rail.

TPS54302 supports4.5–28 V input,3 A output and400 kHz switching; **EN maximum7 V** is respected by3.3 V permission and a local pull-down. Its factory250-piece DDCT reel does not impose a customer MOQ: cut tape1 is the observed procurement form. The original candidate SRP5030TA-100M was rejected because3.5 A saturation is below possible converter fault-current peaks. **SRP7050TA-100M** has4 A thermal /7.5 A saturation rating at25°C and69 mΩ maximum DCR. Its manufacturer land pattern is supplied separately. [TI buck](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps54302.pdf), [Bourns selected inductor](https://www.bourns.com/docs/product-datasheets/srp7050ta.pdf).

At16.8 V/7.96 V/10 µH/400 kHz, nominal ripple is `Vout(1−Vout/Vin)/(L f)=1.05 A pp`; at−20% L it is1.31 A. With an estimated0.85 A output load, peak is1.51 A, well below7.5 A nominal saturation. Actual current-limit tolerance, hot saturation, input diode loss, effective output capacitance and stability remain gates. Two22 µF/25 V output ceramics are **not44 µF guaranteed under bias**. Input10 µF/35 V ceramics and100 nF bypass are named in the BOM. SMBJ17A is provisional: its nominal27.6 V clamp is close to the buck's operating ceiling. Capture real cable transients; no clamp rating substitutes for that test.

Feedback uses typical0.596 V reference,137 kΩ upper and20 kΩ base lower resistor. Latched selections connect91 kΩ or24.9 kΩ in parallel with the lower resistor:

| PWR1/PWR0 sampled at arm | Initial pre-shaper rail | Intended calibrated output |
| --- | ---: | --- |
|00 |4.679 V | Sprint0.85 W initially; independently calibrate within0.3–1 W |
|01 |5.576 V | Extended Classic1.2 W |
|10 |7.958 V | Full Classic3 W; calibration aim3.15 W so uncertainty can establish≥3 W |
|11 | Buck would be8.855 V from the resistor network | **Hardware fault; no operation permitted** |

These are calculated voltages, **not predicted measured RF powers**. Calibrate fixed E96 feedback resistors per mode, with driver≥4.0 V and permitted pre-shaper rail≤8.6 V. Power depends on drive, shaper drop, transformer, filter, load and device temperature; no linear-voltage assumption. Supply selection is held until rearm, preventing GPIO glitches from changing an armed PA rail. Driver supply is unshaped; drain is shaped. LM5134 **B**, not A, accepts3.3 V with2.4 V VIH;4–12.6 V operating limit applies even though14 V is the absolute maximum. Its input rating is independent of VDD; local47 kΩ pulls prevent a floating input when the source disappears.

TPS22810 after the buck provides an actual rail-disconnect element independently of buck switching state. It is **not reverse-blocking**: external rail injection is prohibited. QOD through100 Ω discharges the isolated reservoir; CT1 nF is an initial slew value. Retuning/faults mute the driver and remove both enables. Safe driver voltage during a converter-short trip remains unmeasured: the OV threshold is about8.97 V, and fault testing must keep LM5134 VDD≤12.6 V. Current-limited power tests precede unrestricted4S operation.

The shared key-tail receives COMMON_KEY, the OR of permitted PA_KEY and FOX_KEY. One branch is permitted at a time. Driver INB has its own rail pull-up and **two series sinks**: Q401 requires PA permission, Q405 requires a genuine key-tail signal. This prevents a burst when the drain integrator's capacitor responds to rail startup. Spare HC14/NAND gates implement a Schmitt key-tail from a Schottky-charged100 nF capacitor and330 kΩ discharge resistor. Q406 resets that memory through1 kΩ when ARMED is lost, preventing stale key state on rearm. Qualify **10–80 ms hold**, longer than the measured3–8 ms RF decay, including diode leakage and Schmitt thresholds at temperature. The Fox RF NAND enable also requires this tail, avoiding an armed-but-unkeyed startup pulse while preserving its falling envelope. A fault removes permission immediately. Wait at least250 ms after arming before the first key and verify the drain has settled; this new support addresses an identified circuit startup behavior, not duplicated controller timing.

## Drain shaping, current and thermal calculations

BD140 emitter=VPA_LOAD, collector=VPA_SHAPED, base=SHAPE_BASE;100 Ω/1 W key resistor to a logic-level DMN2056U sink,1 kΩ emitter-base pull-up and22 µF nonpolar integration capacitor. This implements the documented PNP integrator; its new capacitance and operating point require measured3–8 ms RF edges. No large PA reservoir follows the shaper. The driver decoupling is upstream.

For3.15 W, assumed60% drain efficiency and7.6 V drain give0.691 A. At7.96 V rail, pessimistic1.0 V VBE gives `(7.96−1.0)/100−1.0/1000=68.6 mA` base drive: forced beta10.1. Key resistor dissipation is0.484 W; at8.98 V the calculation is0.637 W. Its2512/1 W rating must still be temperature-derated. At an assumed0.5 V pass drop, steady shaper loss is0.346 W. A resistive edge surrogate gives approximately `Vs I/4=1.38 W` peak; this is **not the switching-PA SOA trajectory**. Measure collector current and VCE during every edge/mismatch, apply hot repetitive SOA, and bound junction temperature. ST BD140 has80 V/1.5 A limits,10 K/W junction-case and100 K/W junction-ambient reference figures; at0.5 W/50°C the simple free-air estimate is100°C. It is not a thermal pass.

The referenceMPS751 alternative would have less useful thermal margin and weaker pulse evidence; it is not populated. The 2 m modulator remains untouched. [ST BD140 datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/bd140.pdf).

R3300.05 Ω high-side shunt and INA180A1 gain20 produce1 V/A. A filtered comparator trips nominal 1.251 A. The input pair uses 10 Ω/10 nF to reject RF; a 30 kΩ/10 kΩ output divider produces 0.25 V/A, and its 1 nF capacitor gives 7.5 µs filtering. The independently divided trip reference is 0.312657 V. Require≤100 µs complete fault mute, or a faster limit if hot SOA demands it. Grounded instruments cannot bypass this high-side shunt. Sensor pins are **1 OUT,2 GND,3 IN+,4 IN−,5 VS**. The T packaging is selected because the cheaper R package had contradictory/OOS stock. Price at ten remains estimated. [INA180](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ina180.pdf).

TL431A provides the regulated 2.495 V reference, biased from 3.3 V through 180 Ω. Voltage and current thresholds each have independent dividers from this node. Pack and PA signals divide by 31.9, keeping them below 0.878 V even at 28 V. Nominal protected-input UV thresholds are 5.810 V common and 11.070 V for the 4S backstop; PA OV is 8.973 V. Add the measured reverse-barrier drop to obtain raw-pack thresholds. These are backstops, not replacements for controller operational endpoints of 6.0/12.0 V or external cell-level BMS protection. The thermal hub uses 1.5 kΩ/1 kΩ and is about 0.897–0.940 V under its varying loads; both thermal comparisons use ratios from that same hub. The design calculation establishes at least 2.10 mA TL431 cathode current at the stated worst low supply after branch loads, and comparator inputs below 0.941 V versus the conservative 1.135 V common-mode ceiling at 3.135 V supply. No capacitor is placed directly across the TL431. Reference stability, offsets, input bias and tolerance remain physical gates. See [reproducible calculations](M3_80M_CALCULATIONS.json). Thermal divider10 kΩ/NTC10 kΩ (B25/50=3380 K; approximate trip calculation uses3434 K25/85 reference) with60.4 kΩ/10 kΩ reference gives nominal case trip near80°C. Second comparator detects an open sensor; a short trips hot. **TH301 must be thermally attached to the insulated IRF610 case**, not merely placed somewhere on the board. Its wire/attachment and thermal lag are unquoted ancillary work. Fox-only replaces TH301 with R33610 kΩ, not both.

Aim heat sink **≤10 K/W**, insulated from the drain tab, and measure TJ≤110°C at50°C ambient. The €2 sink/interface/attachment provision is an estimate; exact mechanical MPN/SOA sign-off remains open. A minimum duty cycle does not remove continuous beacon or edge stress.

## Foxoring branch and its limits

R50127 Ω/1 W feeds the PNP integrator; Q501MMBT3906 shapes VFOX. Q503MMBT3904 collector pull-up to VFOX and a Schottky Baker clamp translate the clock, so the AC00 clock input follows its own supply rather than injecting3.3 V during the ramp. R50510 kΩ limits source drive to roughly0.26 mA; Si5351's smallest2 mA drive setting is adequate in this **calculation**, loaded edge quality remains a test. RC and saturation delay affect duty cycle.

Three SN74AC00DR NAND outputs have **individual 22 Ω ballast**, rather than directly tied push-pull pins, and own DC block/elliptic filter/calibration pi pad. Gate enable follows VFOX through Q504; both Fox permission and a genuine shared key-tail are required. This independently masks RF during startup and even if the shaping transistor shorts. The spare gate's inputs are grounded. No watt output selector or relay exists. The PA converter, driver and final are entirely unnecessary in Fox-only population.

The HC00 option was evaluated at 3.3 V. Its CMOS input thresholds and low input-current requirements are compatible, but its small-current output guarantees and the loss of shaped rail voltage leave little conducted-power margin. For example, a 3.0 V rail, 20 Ω internal resistance per gate, original 47 Ω ballast, 0.7 dB filter loss and a 2.4 dB pad give only about **8.5 mW**. That internal resistance is an assumption, not a datasheet guarantee. Therefore select **SN74AC00DR**, retaining the TinyFox NAND mechanism but adapting the drive family, ballast and fixed pad. It costs €0.278 versus €0.103 for HC00 at ten. The DR reel/cut-tape device is Active; the D tube SKU's end-of-life listing is not substituted. [TI AC00 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74ac00.pdf), [current price/stock](https://eu.mouser.com/en/ProductDetail/Texas-Instruments/SN74AC00DR?qs=sGAEpiMZZMuyBeSSR239IT5Ta765RsfINkXV%252B0YV3Y4%3D).

AC00 recommends 2–6 V supply and ±12 mA output operation at 3 V, with 2.1/0.9 V input thresholds. Rail-following inputs address the level requirement. Input and output conditions below 2 V during shaping remain uncharacterized. Measure the loaded amplitude, each gate current, clock edges, envelope and supply ramp; do not infer nominal RF power from drive-current ratings.

For the selected branch, R501 is **27 Ω/1 W**, ballast is **22 Ω per gate**, and the starting 50 Ω pi pad is **750 Ω shunts /6.8 Ω series**, about **1.17 dB** ideally. Fixed calibration may increase attenuation; **at least 1 dB** is required by the fault budget. A 2.6 V rail and assumed 20 Ω internal resistance per gate give about **10.8 mW after 0.7 dB filter loss and the starting pad**. This is a feasibility calculation, not a guarantee: rail drop, actual output impedance, filter input reactance and gate-current peaks need measurements. If 9–11 mW cannot be achieved with all ratings/shaping/fault limits, stop for the retained BJT-buffer fallback review; do not silently raise the supply or reuse the watt PA.

With the valid controller maximum 3.6 V, the maximum extractable power through 27 Ω is `V²/(4R)=120 mW`. Two conservative input-drive budgets add `2×V²/10k=2.6 mW`; minimum 1 dB available-power attenuation gives **less than 98.4 mW available fault power**, including −1% feed/input resistor tolerance. Qualify the pad loss and tolerance across the fault spectrum/load cases, not just nominal matched S21. This is not nominal 10 mW, a spectral result, or a claim that a damaged logic chip survives every short. The compact network must withstand **100 mW fault input plus measured stored-energy transients**. The 1 W feed resistor withstands `3.6²/27=0.48 W` under a short. Pad bypass, raw battery injection into J101 and deliberately miswired external adapters are outside this bound and prohibited. Normal PA copper cannot route to J502; layout/near-field isolation still needs testing. An RF detector is omitted conditionally because of this bounded source, independent RF mute and separate port; external calibration and physical fault tests remain mandatory.

J301 VH2:1 pack positive,2 dedicated power return. J401 screw terminal:1 normal RF,2 RF return. J502 XH3:1 Fox RF,2 compact return,3 accessory-presence short to2. Dissimilar shells prevent direct mating. J502 does not guarantee sequential contacts: **disarm before plugging**; its presence trips normal permission independently. No raw voltage travels in the IDC harness. Proposed logic budget≤100 mA steady/200 mA startup, normal pack branch≤1.5 A; these are connector/fixture requirements, not measured currents. Dedicated raw return and short RF current loops meet at a controlled ground point; measure any current diverted through the control-ground harness.

One dedicated HF I²C bus is preferred: each module's Si5351 has0x60, and any local SCL activity trips HF. Do not assume unrestricted shared HF/VHF bus traffic while HF is armed. No I²C isolation switch is needed **on this RF module** because all source/control logic uses the same always-powered3.3 V domain; controller/fixture must not drive bus lines when that domain is absent. The source powers down PLL/outputs during idle when validated; no unmeasured standby saving is booked.

HC123 timing capacitors connect to grounded CEXT pins 6/14 as recommended for noise immunity; D201/D202 clamp their RC nodes to V3V3 during power-down, following the manufacturer application diagram. Qualify discharge current, supply collapse and lease timeout at temperature. Healthy inputs combine POR, lease, bus quiet, MUTE, common UV, PA OV/OC, thermal window, exclusive requests, valid mode and request-conditioned pack/cartridge permission. ARM low asynchronously clears; delayed rising ARM samples the modes and armed latch. Loss of heartbeat, reset/brownout, disconnected controller, any fault or retune clears ARMED, immediately mutes RF and disconnects normal rail. Returning health with ARM held high **does not rearm**. The circuit is not safety-certified or redundant against every logic-IC failure. It provides independent fail-off for the stated controller/reset/clock/supply faults, subject to the physical gates.

Startup:3.3 V stable/POR complete; ARM/KEY/requests/MUTE low; configure/verify Si5351; choose path/mode and validated pack; ensure both dummy loads/accessories appropriate; MUTE high, regular software-progress lease, ARM low≥20 ms then high; wait≥250 ms before keying. Key-up retains the source/driver until the measured envelope tail completes. Before retune, disarm/MUTE low; any unannounced SCL transition also trips hardware. Emergency shutdown can truncate an edge. Communications loss is covered by the **progress lease contract**; hardware detects SCL activity but does not authenticate bus transactions/frequency readback.

## Remaining electrical decisions

Before an evaluation PCB: close active-part/local stock and passive voltage/footprint reviews; sign off transformer winding polarity; confirm bulk effective capacitance/stability and OV transient protection calculations; attach the NTC reliably; review3–8 ms shaping starts on both branches and the Fox current/output uncertainty. Generic schematic ERC cannot close these. Low-cost external loads, current logger, probes and spectrum instruments replace onboard meters; the [test plan](M3_80M_TEST_PLAN.md) specifies the physical go/no-go gates.
