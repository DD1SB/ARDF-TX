# M5A-R1 — smallest decisive PA/AM coupon

Date: **2026-10-15**. **M5A-R1 electrical evaluation candidate — NOT RELEASED. All physical tests NOT_RUN.**

**No PCB is laid out or authorized for fabrication.** The coupon layout is currently NO-GO until readable original ST circuit/package drawings close the named topology/pad/land blockers. Thereafter a small standalone PA coupon, not the integrated transmitter PCB, is the next useful engineering step. Its purpose is to settle final-stage gain, drain-to-RF transfer, stability and crest capability. It may omit the controller/source/watchdog because laboratory enable/current limits control this isolated fixture; the integrated R1 schematic retains full-system interlocks.

## Coupon scope and interfaces

- One PD54008L-E, exact verified Power FLAT land and source thermal contact, removable/tunable 144 MHz input and output matches, gate stopper/bias isolation, output LPF and 50 Ω SMA reference port. Original ST final/output topology must first be traced; do not manufacture the OPEN assumed-impedance harness.
- Separate 50 Ω drive input for a laboratory generator. The PHA-1+ driver may be evaluated on a removable adjoining fixture later; no need to populate both complete alternative MMICs. Keep a calibrated interstage measurement plane and DC block. Integrated board test injection uses C602 omitted and TP604/PA_IN50; never connect a source across an active MMIC output.
- Independent adjustable gate bias with default ground, measurable drain current and separate bounded drain supply. External modulated supply can characterize AM before D45H11/BD139 or a battery converter is fitted. Start gate trim grounded; constrain VGS from−0.5to+15 V, and use a much lower conservative practical drive/bias limit.
- Target the original 0.508 mm FR4/70 µm construction, supplier-declared dielectric/mask. Recalculate every changed microstrip width/length/launch. No0.92 mm trace copied to 1.6 mm FR4. RF ground/vias, exposed source land, thermal attachment and tunable-value pads must be reviewed before routing approval.
- Kelvin DC current connection, drain RF choke/bypass arrangement verified for AM bandwidth, removable load/filter links, thermocouple attachment and short RF probe ground. No large capacitor on the modulated drain. External lab enclosure/attenuation prevents unintended radiation.

## Shared instruments

Current-limited 0–12 V lab supply with bounded overshoot, gate-bias supply, RF generator with calibrated output (external laboratory booster if more drive is needed), VNA with bias isolation, spectrum analyzer through rated 40–60 dB attenuation, calibrated≥10W50Ω dummy load, carrier/PEP power meter, oscilloscope with RF envelope detector, active RF probes≤0.5 pF where needed and appropriate differential voltage probes and current/temperature logging. Use a calibrated directional coupler for mismatch/reflected power. Account for load/filter/cable loss and detector response. Bench connections are conducted into loads; no antenna/RF transmission authorization is implied.

## Decisive outcome

Accept the economical chain only if the same configuration simultaneously closes gain/backoff, connector carrier/PEP, full 70–80% RF sine AM, spectral, thermal and supply gates. If required gain is too low, use the stronger driver basket and recompute heat/endurance. If the low-voltage final cannot provide the RF envelope or crest safely, return to preserved R0; do not lower the competition requirement to save money. Document raw instrument files, calibration planes, firmware/generator settings, temperature and uncertainty. No test is PASS until actual data exists.

Thermal instruments must have insulated junctions or appropriate isolation: D45H11 and BD139 exposed collectors are different live nets. Never infer PA junction temperature from plastic-top temperature using a source-land RθJC without the actual package heat-flow definition. Use de-embedded couplers at50Ω ports; an ordinary10pF probe can substantially change144MHz matching. A separateDC-coupled modulation amplifier/supply must provide the proposed0–8V envelope at≥2A with sufficient audio bandwidth (initially≥20kHz) and bounded current; an ordinary bench supply is not assumed to follow800Hz. Use insulated thermocouple junctions/isolated logging on live tabs.
