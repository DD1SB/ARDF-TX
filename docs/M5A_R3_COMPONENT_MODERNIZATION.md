## Physical pin audit versus actual footprint readiness

[Machine-readable pin audit](../hardware/2m/review/M5A-R3/pinout-audit.json) checks **31 numeric schematic device maps** against actual exported nets. It explicitly separates verified terminal numbers from approved PCB landings. Mutations swapping ASI base/collector, Central emitter/collector, TIP127 base/emitter and BF981 gates must fail.

**No RF power footprint is guessed.** Q901/Q902/Q903 lack approved footprints. SOT103 lead trimming, original TO39 height/cooling and exact final manufacturer must be resolved together. The ASI drawing does not authorize placing a different MRF237 maker on its pin numbering. TIP127 tab is modulatedVPA; use electrical insulation from ground/other live devices and account for interface thermal resistance. Choke/trimmer/connector geometry and TPS611781 RNW landing are also OPEN.

There are19 unset physical footprint fields, including optional populations; ERC's warning count is smaller because some custom symbols have no footprint filter. **An unflagged custom symbol is not a qualified footprint.** The PDF/netlist are electrically reviewable; a manufacture-ready PCB is not defensible yet.
