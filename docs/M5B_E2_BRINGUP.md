# M5B-E2 single-board commissioning

**All stages are NOT_RUN. This plan does not authorize purchasing, assembly, power-up or transmission.** After separate approvals, populate one board progressively. The detailed [E1 procedure](M5B_E1_BRINGUP.md) remains applicable; the E2 additions below form one integrated build/acceptance sequence. Record serial, instruments/calibration, conditions, uncertainty, operator and measured results in `acceptance-register.csv`.

Use isolated adjustable current-limited supplies, DMM, oscilloscope with suitable low-inductance/differential probes, RF envelope detector, frequency/spectrum analyzer, RF carrier/PEP measurement, protected attenuators/coupler, 50 Ω ≥10 W dummy load and temperature logging. A VNA is useful for initial input alignment. Protect instruments from RF power and DC; ordinary long-ground-lead probing can detune or damage VHF nodes.

1. **Unpowered inspection.** Confirm native checksum, exact manufacturers, pin1/keying, PD gate/drain/source, PHA RF/ground order, isolated RNW6/7/8, ST B1/C2/E3 and live tabs. Inspect the PACK_OK_N correction, four control junctions and 0.4 mm GND notch. Verify shorts, supply continuity, correct converter links/IDs/dividers, finished holes, source paste/via tents and thermal insulation. Mount supports before stressed lead soldering. Hidden PowerFLAT/RNW/WSON pads require controlled reflow and appropriate joint inspection.

2. **Low-voltage logic.** Battery absent; 3.3 V initially limited to 100 mA. ARM/SLOT/KEY/PREPARE/PA_PREPARE low, MUTE_N low. Increase only within the 150 mA logic budget after inspection. Verify reset, genuine progress edges, watchdog loss, I²C activity/stuck bus, invalid ARM/path requests, captured profile, fault latch and deliberate rearming. Held ARM must not restart automatically. Configure/read back a legal source frequency without RF; championship range is 144.500–144.900 MHz. The future common controller owns legal-band configuration and scheduling.

3. **Bounded power supply.** Fit exactly one BOOST2S or BUCK4S population, matching J302 shunt/R101/dividers. TRAIN_ONLY omits both normal converters and PA. Leave U501 absent and R952 open. Start 2S at 6.5 V/0.1 A or 4S at 12.5 V/0.1 A with short monitored leads. Check wrong-pack inhibit, UV/OV, startup/shutdown and rails. Nominal UV thresholds are 6.21/12.15 V; 6.0/12.0 V should inhibit. Full upstream rail is approximately 9.021 V, reduced4S approximately 7.510 V within real tolerances.

   After low-energy checks, permit raw input up to 3.5 A for 2S or 2 A for 4S as required. The regulated electronic-load ceiling is ≤2 A; the local approximately 1.797 A trip must remain active. INA output should approach 2.443 V at nominal trip. Do not confuse raw 2S current with regulated-rail current.

   Apply a 0–180 mA driver dummy load at VDRV_RF/TP401 ahead of open R952. Require equivalent MMIC feed 4.8–5.2 V, acceptable dropout, clean startup and discharge. Test each regulator-short case separately using a bounded fixture, MMIC absent. Maximum fault is 16.8 V **at the tested pin**; initially ≤100 µs, ≤0.25 A, ≤1 pulse/s, hence ≤0.42 mJ source energy. Require MMIC node ≤5.7 V, correct inhibit/latch and no restart. U950's 16.8 V fault exceeds its 16 V recommended input although below 18 V absolute maximum: bound duration/current and inspect afterward. A surviving LDO could dissipate about 2.1 W continuously; do not hold this fault indefinitely. Measure isolation time rather than assuming 20 µs. Test hotplug/disconnect with a bounded laboratory source before unrestricted pack use. Do not fit U501/R952 if these tests fail.

4. **AM modulator, no RF.** Use a 5.6 Ω ≥25 W representative load and insulated pass/helper mounting. Verify feedback polarity, loop stability, crest dropout, startup/shutdown, current/thermal inhibit and reconstructed sine; initial PWM ripple target ≤1% of carrier envelope. Full 0.8/4.0/7.2 V and reduced 0.444/2.222/4.0 V trough/center/crest are voltage starting points, not RF AM calibration. Require pass case ≤60°C at defined worst ambient, measured NTC trip near nominal 65.85°C with tolerance/lag, and deliberate rearm after cooling. Check sensor attachment; intact-wire physical detachment is not detected electrically. Simulate pass faults only with bounded energy.

5. **Normal RF and one alignment.** Fit the MMIC only after rail qualification. Open R650 and temporarily fit R651 49.9 Ω for protected 50 Ω driver measurement. Begin with the 10 dB source pad; select documented 6/8/10 dB options aiming at approximately 19 dBm and about 3 dB compression headroom. Record a drive deficit rather than silently lowering ERP requirements.

   Remove R651, restore R650, fit final/filter/grounded thermal assembly and connect normal SMA to the dummy load. Start at low drive/drain voltage; initial bias-only Idq 50 mA, with >200 mA requiring documented justification. Use only bounded input options: L634 24/33/47 nH; C631 DNP/22/33/47/56 pF; C633 DNP/22/27/33/39/47 pF. Record the final population. ST output matching/filter values remain unchanged. Check 144.500/144.700/144.900 MHz and any claimed legal training limits.

   Measure carrier, average RF, PEP and actual envelope depth at the connector. Require 70–80% AM, nominal75%; initial engineering distortion target ≤5% THD and sideband asymmetry ≤1 dB. At assumed −1 dBd feed/antenna gain, 1 W ERP needs 1.259 W conducted carrier and 4.079 W PEP at80% AM. Actual gain and calibration uncertainty remain to be measured. A nominal0.9 W ERP with0.4 dB total uncertainty gives an upper bound about0.987 W; reduced0.30 W needs its own calibration. Harmonic engineering target is ≤−60 dBc or the stricter national mask. Initial off-state targets are normal ≤−70 dBm, training ≤−90 dBm or instrument floor; they are not literal zero-RF proof. Record no-tone operation, spurs, temperatures and current. Test mismatch only with bounded reduced energy.

6. **Training and isolation.** Main PA off; use the independent MMCX 50 Ω reference. Calibrate nominal1 µW carrier (−30 dBm), true AM and timing. Measure startup/off-state leakage and normal-to-training coupling under controlled normal-output load/fault conditions. The watt stage must not feed the compact connector. TRAIN_ONLY must operate with normal PA, driver, converter and thermal hardware DNP and without backpower paths.

7. **Operational acceptance.** Repeat controller-loss, heartbeat, reset, I²C, invalid-request, UV/OV/current/thermal and deliberate-rearm tests. Verify legal frequency, scheduling and off-slot silence. Log continuous/no-tone and contest-duty thermal/current behavior. Measure 12 h full and24 h reduced endurance with defined20% duty, controller/LoRa loads, temperature, aging and cutoff; analyze continuous beacons separately. Antenna/feed ERP, horizontal pattern and field acceptance remain required before use.

No calculation, original ST measurement or CAD check counts as a physical acceptance result for this board.
