Gate B: defined prototype process, not a claim of qualified source solder. Use a commercial laser-cut304stainless **0.12 mm source-only stencil**, which is a [standard JLC thickness](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-stencil-manufacturing). The separate stencil directory contains native-derived Q501 source and G/D paste artwork only; it is not part of the bare-board fabrication ZIP. Leave stencil apertures as supplied; no automatic enlargement, paste added into holes or blind backfilling. Other components may be hand soldered/staged with their appropriate stencil; the0.12 mm local stencil is not a blanket validation of every fine-pitch aperture elsewhere. No step stencil or factory dispensing is required.

Use SAC305 Type4 no-clean low-voiding stencil paste, for example AIMV9, with its actual lot TDS/SDS and storage/expiry followed. The [manufacturer sheet](https://www.aimsolder.com/wp-content/uploads/v9_solder_paste_tds.pdf) is a material example; its low-void performance claims are not our measured result. An equivalent paste requires rechecking the profile and first joint. Buy a practical small pack when separately authorized; current small-pack availability and cost are unquoted. Do not mix expired/dried paste or hand-add flux to alter its solids loading.

1. Inspect bare board and exact Q501identity/orientation. Under magnification check full source opening, no islands, no bridges, open joint holes and continuous mask separation toward side/end holes. Measure a sample of actual hole sizes/positions; the nominal tolerance envelope is not a via-size guarantee. Check HASL uniformity, flatness and no balls on the bottom contact. Reject badly crowned/contaminated lands. No ENIG substitution is silently assumed. Clean accessible land with electronics-gradeIPA/lint-free wipe, then dry fully. Follow device moisture label/bake guidance if needed; do not invent a universal bake temperature.
2. Reflow Q501before installing the bar/sink or nearby tall parts. Support the PCB flat on ordinary edge supports; leave the source holes vented and the underside clear. Tape/align the local stencil under10–20×magnification to the visible G/Dland edges. Make one controlled squeegee pass; lift vertically. Inspect all five separate deposits and G/D deposits for registration, bridges, smears, missing print and paste in holes. Clean/reprint a bad deposit rather than topping it up. Archive a magnified print photograph and actual foil thickness. Nominal paste volume0.8118 mm³ can fall to0.292–0.487 mm³ solid solder under the stated transfer/solids sensitivities; it is not a guaranteed joint volume.
3. Place the device gently with fine tweezers under magnification, aligning Gleft/Dright and the5 mm body to the lands. Aim for±0.05 mm, do not press paste into holes or load the plastic. This manual method departs from ST's preferred machine placement and must be qualified on the initial build. Use a controlled small reflow oven, or a profiled hot plate with controlled top heating if needed. An iron on the outer leads alone cannot establish the hidden source joint.
4. Measure temperature with a fine thermocouple at the Q501land region on the first board; oven/plate display temperature is not the joint temperature. Adjust to actual paste/device limits and demonstrate that both source and terminals reflow without excessive gradient. The exact RFpart's pack label and solder-paste TDS govern; never exceed260 °C or10snear that maximum. These are initial engineering settings, not a measured profile or authorization to heat hardware. Do not fit heatsink/bar during reflow or use a torch.
5. Cool without moving the board/device until solder solidifies. Inspect alignment, G/Dbridges, balls, tilt, source-edge wetting where visible and bottom solder protrusion. Clean residues according to the chosen paste; do not flood an uncleanable hidden joint with corrosive flux. Check source-to-GNDcontinuity and gate/drain isolation as appropriate for the actual transistor diode/circuit behavior. Record the test voltage/polarity; never apply arbitrary megohmmeter voltage to semiconductor gates. Visual edges/DCcontinuity cannot determine hidden voids or full wetting.
6. **Initial qualification:**outsource one populated Q501board for X-ray after the actual recorded print/reflow. Evaluate projected joint area, void location, bridges and solder loss/tilt; holes must not be mistaken for solder voids. Project acceptance starts at≥4 mm² effective wet area and≤0.10 mm standoff with no dominant central void; X-ray area alone cannot establish metallurgical wetting or thermal uniformity. Correlate with bounded electrical/thermal measurements before raising PAenergy. If evidence disagrees, use controlled removal/sectioning of one spare identical integrated board/site, not a new PA-coupon program. Requalify after paste, finish, stencil, reflow or substantial process changes. Once correlated, the ordinary builder uses the qualified record plus visual/electrical/profile/thermal checks; industrial X-ray is not assumed on every fleet board. Until then Gate DremainsNOT_RUN.
7. Rework only with controlled bottom preheat/top hot air, monitoring actual package temperatures. Lift only after all joints melt; never lever on source copper. Remove residue and dress excess solder without filling or damaging holes, inspect pads, then reprint/reflow with a replacement part as needed. A source-joint rework reopens its qualification record; do not inject solder through vias to hide starvation. Follow the exact device's allowed reflow exposure; discard damaged pads/parts rather than claiming a standard repair count.
