# M5B-E4R1 source-joint construction

Implemented candidate: **hybrid16 intentionally open under-joint vias,20 side vias,8 end vias and54 retained wing vias** (98total). Q501 remains at(112,55); ST G/D/S copper lands and all signal conductors are unchanged. Source opening is2.1×5 mm inside the2.7×5 mm copper land. There are no source mask islands and no closure guarantee. Side lands0.60/0.30 have0.15 mm nominal annuli, an ordinary-process preferred-margin exception; joint/end lands0.80/0.30 and wing lands1.10/0.50 remain standard through vias. The side rails widen local GND3.2→3.6 mm; nominal gate/drain clearance is0.20 mm. End holes move away from the paste interface to(110.8+0.8c,52/58), c0..3. The old end rows at52.3/57.7 could encroach on the joint under hole/registration sensitivity.

The [exact ST datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/pd54008l-e.pdf), January2007Rev2, p13Table11, illustrates18vias in3×6 beneath the source. It dimensions the lands but does **not** prescribe a via bore or pitch. We retain its land/pin authority. [AN5046](https://www.st.com/resource/en/application_note/an5046-printed-circuit-board-assembly-recommendations-for-stmicroelectronics-powerflat-packages-stmicroelectronics.pdf), Rev4March2019, provides broader PowerFLAT assembly guidance: closed vias are preferred and the central paste deposit is divided. Its5×6 test packages and assembly results do not qualify this5×5RF/HASL/open-via derivative. It discourages manual placement; our magnified manual placement is an explicit prototype deviation requiring first-build qualification. AN1703's generic powerMOS5×5 central-drain package must not replace this RF part's central-source map.

All alternatives use the same copper, bar, TIM, sink, joint and mesh boundary assumptions in the recalculated comparison; originalE4numbers are separately retained rather than mixed with the changed solver. No separate PA board or new RF architecture is proposed. Each alternative needs the same basic reflow equipment and initial joint inspection; none is physically qualified. B is simplest to print/repair but thermally poorest; C is cheapest in artwork changes but most wicking-sensitive; A retains the closest illustrated count; D gives the best practical compromise at unchanged PCB process and cooling cost.

For this common conservative 10 µm/40 °C/4 W case, the updated model predicts approximately134 °C forA,137 °C forB,114 °C forC, and122 °C forD.The CSV comparison records via counts, nominal areas and resistance. RejectedA/Cpaste details are not represented as completed or qualified designs.

KiCad's automatic front via-land mask circles are suppressed locally. The explicit continuous source opening exposes all16joint holes in the actual Gerber, so these holes are physically open even though the per-via front-tent flag suppresses automatic annulus apertures. This is deliberate mask artwork, not a claim of tenting or closure. `source-Gerber-geometry-audit.json` checks the exported opening and five paste windows against native coordinates. No0.45 mm islands remain.

Native source mask area is10.50 mm². Subtracting nominal16×0.30holes leaves **9.37 mm²** solderable copper (not actual solder coverage). Using0.43 mm holes as a sensitivity leaves8.18 mm². JLC's current via note says via sizes are not controlled like component holes; the PTH tolerance is a sensitivity, not a guaranteed via bound. Inspect incoming holes/registration. The side-hole-to-opening separation is0.11 mm in the stated0.43hole/±0.075position/±0.05mask sensitivity; this is a mask web to the solder area, not a hole cap. A failed tent away from the opening does not invalidate the process. Aperture/hole overlap can still occur within manufacturing/printing variation; zero wicking is not assumed.

Five rectangular paste apertures total **6.765 mm²**,50.1%of the manufacturer's13.5 mm² land. Outer apertures2.05×0.75, inner three2.05×0.60;0.40 mm gaps aligned to the nominal via rows. Minimum area ratio is1.93at0.12 mm foil, well above0.66. Geometric paste volume is0.8118 mm³;80–100%transfer and45–60%metal volume give only0.292–0.487 mm³ solid solder. These are engineering sensitivities, not paste-vendor guarantees. The open16-hole volume is1.81 mm³, larger than the solder deposit: uncontrolled full wicking would fail. Apertures avoid nominal holes; they cannot prevent all redistribution during reflow.

The process is credible for an evaluation board because it uses ordinary small open thermal vias, readily released segmented deposits, controlled reflow and explicit first-joint qualification. It is not analytically guaranteed. Wetting area, standoff, void location and retained solder must be established physically. The conditional1 K/W joint envelope corresponds to≥4 mm² effective wet contact,≤0.10 mm thickness andk≥25 W/mK; that scalar envelope cannot bound a central hot-spot void. X-ray plus thermal correlation must assess distribution, not only total area. Build and inspection instructions are in [manufacturing](M5B_E4R1_MANUFACTURING.md). Rework requires removing Q501 with controlled preheat, cleaning and reprinting; no blind solder injection or credited solder-filled holes.
