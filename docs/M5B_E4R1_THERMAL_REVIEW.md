# M5B-E4R1 thermal review — calculated, not measured

At40 °C ambient/4 W continuous PAheat, the updated model gives approximately **91 °C nominal 18 µm**, **122 °C conservative 10 µm** and **138 °C poor-joint/contact 10 µm**. The conservative 110 °C design target is **NOT_MET**. The preferred100 °C nominal target is met only conditionally. The125 °C engineering gate remains **OPEN**; physical uncertainty and degraded contact can exceed it. This is not unrestricted4 W operation approval. Neither reduced contest duty nor lower assumed PAheat is used to disguise the4 W qualification case.

`thermal-model.json`/`thermal-sensitivity.csv` contain25/40 °C,2/3/4/5 W,10/15/18/25 µm barrels, loss of4/8joint vias, joint resistance, TIM, contact loss, sink orientation and upper board-thickness sensitivities. The10 µm case is conservative sensitivity, not a JLC minimum guarantee. OriginalE4predictions80.7/109.5/121.7 °C are preserved as historical model outputs. The new comparison reruns bothE4andE4R1with identical new boundary assumptions. It adds hole-area exclusion and circumferential via coupling, and uses the actual reduced solder opening. Therefore the difference from oldE4is partly model correction, not just lost vias.

The model solves two coupled copper sheets, with each via barrel linking corresponding circumference cells and distributed TIM to an isothermal bar. Removed hole area reduces sheet and contact conductance. It credits neither solder filling, plastic-top cooling, FR4conduction nor free-board convection. Source surface is assumed isothermal over the opening; real die/solder hot spots remain unknown. Rjc3 K/W is the exact ST rating; source joint, bar interfaces and sink terms are separate series elements after the solved source-to-bar path. No extra spreading resistance is added twice. Parallel vias and lateral sheets are solved as a network, not summed as unrelated series terms.

Nominal:35µmCu,18 µm barrels,0.05 mm/10 W m⁻¹K⁻¹TIM, joint0.25, bar/second-interface0.25, sink2.2 K/W, no lostvias. Conservative:30µmCu,10 µm barrels,0.15 mm/6 W m⁻¹K⁻¹TIM, joint1, bar0.7, sink4 K/W, four joint vias ineffective. All reported totals add1 K/W numerical allowance. Poor assembly uses0.25 mm/5TIM, joint1.5, sink5.5, eight lostvias and70%contact. None of these assembly values is manufacturer-guaranteed. The0.15 mm grease line is intentionally pessimistic; halving it with ordinary flatness/control gives only a modest improvement and does not recover110 °C. Enlarging the sink does not repair a starved source joint and is not selected.

The0.40/0.20/0.10/0.05 mm meshes and solver residuals are recorded. Selected source-to-barR at0.10→0.05changes **0.02 K/W**, or0.1 °C at4 W. Discretization is not monotonically convergent at every coarse grid because hole boundaries and source edges cross cells. Report temperatures rounded to whole degrees for engineering decisions, not tenths as evidence of a target pass. Geometry, voids, barrel plating and real convection produce larger unknowns than solver residuals.
