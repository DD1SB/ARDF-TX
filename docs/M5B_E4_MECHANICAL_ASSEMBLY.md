# M5B-E4 mechanical assembly — candidate instructions, not authorization

Use [top](../hardware/2m/review/M5B-E4/mechanical-top.svg), [bottom](../hardware/2m/review/M5B-E4/mechanical-bottom.svg) and [section](../hardware/2m/review/M5B-E4/mechanical-section.svg). Coordinates are the native KiCad coordinates. The assembly is on HOLD for source-joint qualification; these instructions prepare engineering review only.

PA sink: **Fischer SK81 100 SA**,100×100×15mm,4mm base. The [manufacturer profile/curve](https://cdn-reichelt.de/documents/datenblatt/C800/OJSK81_D_E_F.pdf),PDFp14/catalogueA34, gives approximately2.2K/W at100mm length in passive operation. The [retail listing](https://www.reichelt.de/de/de/shop/produkt/kuehlkoerper_100_mm_alu_2_2_k_w-227984) was€7.30 including19%VAT (€6.13 net), listed available2026-10-17; fleet quantity is unconfirmed. Its0.214kg is **packaging weight**, not verified net mass; reserve0.25kg mechanical load. The page's2.0K/W description conflicts with its2.2 title; the model uses the manufacturer graph and a4K/W sensitivity, not the lower description. SK92 100 SA is an available functional cooling alternative100×100×40mm, approximately1.4K/W, but adds25mm height and about0.3kg; it is not automatically mechanically interchangeable.

Orient the board vertically so sink channels run vertically; keep at least20mm clear at channel ends and avoid adjacent heat sources. The bench model assumes free natural convection. No fan or closed-enclosure performance is credited. The100mm square sink fits x62..162/y20..120 below the180×140 board. Total projection below PCB is about25.2mm. Retain40mm corner supports, trimmed leads≤2.5mm and≥5mm clearance to unrelated conductive parts. No final outdoor enclosure is qualified.

Cut ordinary **EN AW6060 T6 aluminum flat bar15×10mm** to44mm. Deburr and check contact flatness≤0.05mm over the14×16 window using a straightedge/feeler; hand lapping on flat glass with abrasive paper is permitted. No precision machining is assumed. Two3.3mm clearance holes are at bar-local(7.5,4)/(7.5,40), matching existing PCBH5/H6(112,32)/(112,68). Drill/tap the sink from its flat face at local(50,12)/(50,48). Use a2.5mm drill and M3 hand tap; allow screw tips into the fin space, never force a bottomed screw. Drilling through the base/fin at two positions is ordinary workshop work. Maintain at least approximately3mm full thread engagement in the4mm base; verify the actual screw stack before tightening.

Use two M3×16 screws with OD9×0.8 wide washers. Nominal engagement is about3.5mm after1.6mm board,10mm bar,0.1mm combined grease films and0.8mm washer; check actual board1.44–1.76 and stock-bar tolerance. Do not use thick extra washers without rechecking engagement. Tighten alternately just enough for even grease witness, proposed initial0.10N·m (engineering starting value, not a vendor torque specification). The board must remain flat against the supported bar; no load is applied to Q501 plastic, contacts or solder joints. Do not pull a bowed PCB flat by tightening. Support the sink/board as one assembly during handling and cable service.

Use **Kerafol KP12** or an independently reviewed equivalent. Its [June2026 manufacturer sheet](https://www.kerafol.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Kerafol_Keratherm_Datenblatt_KP97_98_99_12_14_06-26.pdf) lists10W/mK reference data at25µm; the model derates to6W/mK at150µm. Apply a thin continuous film, not a millimetre-thick gap pad. Grease is **not electrical insulation**. The whole bar footprint plus0.5mm margin is checked against actual bottom foreign copper. Grounding is intentional: an exposed existing top-GND patch at x111..113/y71.8..72.3 contacts the H6 OD9 washer; bare screw/bar and freshly tapped sink threads complete the bond. Verify source-to-sink continuity<0.1Ω and no contact to gate/drain/live power nets. Do not rely on solder mask as insulation or grease as the ground connection.

Before fitting Q501, inspect ordinary via tents and the source land under magnification. Do not add wire inserts, epoxy fill or uncontrolled solder filling. Use a qualified source-joint process only after the HOLD is resolved. Segmented0.15mm stencil slots and0.45mm mask islands are real native geometry, but their presence is not a validated solder process. Clean, dry and inspect board/bar; no solder bumps or burrs may prop the bar away from the contact window. Inspect hidden source solder coverage by an accepted method before full-energy RF.

D45H11 remains pin2/tab **VPA**, BD139 pin2/tab **MOD_SINK**. Neither may share bare metal with PA ground or each other. Use separate supports and genuine insulating interfaces. Fischer **GS220P**,18×12×0.05mm mica, reference0.4K/W plus grease/contacts, and **IB6** sleeve3.5OD/3.1ID/6flange/2.2height are concrete TO220 candidates ([manufacturer mica sheet](https://www.farnell.com/datasheets/3190623.pdf), [IB6 drawing](https://cdn-reichelt.de/documents/datenblatt/C800/FISCHERELEKTRONIK_IB_6.pdf)). Check the actual tab/sleeve stack; the sleeve must prevent screw contact without holding the tab off its mica. Do not force IB6 into BD139's smaller plastic mounting hole. Use GS32P for its exposed collector face and a suitably rated M3 nylon fastener/insulating washer arrangement, with no metal screw touching exposed collector. Exact supplier fastener dimensions/creep are incoming-assembly checks, not qualified by the PCB.

Retain pass-case≤60°C, calculated junction≤125°C and existing approximately65.85°C NTC trip. Do not increase the trip. Bond the existing insulated NTC bead to the pass case with electrically insulating thermal adhesive, thin film and strain relief; exact adhesive/attachment remains unquoted and requires inspection. The unchanged model needs pass sink≤3.5K/W and total interface≤1K/W. A separate SK10075SA(66×75×40mm, catalogue1.9K/W) is a concrete candidate; its external support/lead forming must be checked before population. The present3D model does not qualify that separate mounting. For4W PA heat, illustrative resistive-load AM assumptions give pass loss≈4.24W at9.02V upstream;3.5+1K/W predicts case≈59.1°C at40°C, little margin. Hot DC SOA, helper base-current demand, sensor lag and continuous no-tone behavior remain physical gates. These existing limitations remain visible rather than being hidden in the PA correction.
