# M5B-E6R1 — common-cooler decision

Retain **one Fischer SK81 100 SA** as the mechanical comparator, **245–270 g complete cooling assembly estimate**. Do not enlarge it or release a smaller cooler while PA protection and full-beacon adverse loss/cooling bounds remain open. Exactly three reproducible structures were compared in [numerical data](../hardware/2m/review/M5B-E6R1/cooling-comparison.json).

The SK81 catalogue/index values assume favorable orientation. Reichelt's SK81 100 title and detailed property differ (2.2/2.0 K/W); 2.2 is retained consistently as the E6 comparator, not a guaranteed in-enclosure value. Stock/prices are indexed observations on 21 October 2026, not baskets or quantity quotes. The flat-stock listing's minimum-cut price is not a quote for this 100 mm piece. [Sources and hashes](../hardware/2m/review/M5B-E6R1/REFERENCES.md) and [cost qualifications](M5B_E6R1_COST_IMPACT.md) preserve these distinctions.

At 1 W ERP, 8 WPM, 40°C and nominal C losses, SK81 100 at 2.2/4/5.5 K/W gives PA cycle-mean peaks 79.06/84.53/89.08°C and pass cases 49.62/55.09/59.64°C. SK81 75 at its 6.25 K/W restricted comparator gives pass 61.91°C. The bare plate at 8 K/W gives 67.22°C pass. PA figures need positive AM ripple added. Smaller mass does not remove the local source bottleneck, and the full-profile adverse efficiency/beta/supply cases still exceed the pass criterion on the original 5.5 K/W boundary. Full 1 W beacon capability must remain; a lower ERP option is not the acceptance remedy.

All three devices share **one supported flat plane**. Retain E6's integral Q501 source bar, approximately 15 ×44 mm plan. A practical stock 6 mm thickness plus 0.25 mm source interface, approximately 0.05 mm grease and 1.6 mm board yields a mounting-plane target near Z = −7.9 mm relative to PCB top. This is a concept tolerance stack, not a machining drawing or approved lead-form. Simulation retains the old 6.2 mm bar path and gives no thermal improvement credit for this simplification. Q401 and Q402 would lie toward the same plane through future ordinary PCB clearance/cutout provisions, with all mounting holes and screwdriver paths approved during a later CAD milestone. They are not separate coolers hidden in a shared enclosure.

Q501 source/bar is GND. Q401 tab is VPA and Q402 tab MOD_SINK. Each tab requires a separate rated mica/insulator and shoulder-bush arrangement; no solder-mask, grease or anodizing insulation credit. E6 insulator/interface resistance assumptions remain unchanged here. Ground the extrusion through a controlled bond and support it with independent brackets/standoffs using the preserved 140 ×100 mm mounting pattern. Do not load transistor leads, Q501 solder or bend the PCB under screw pressure. Retain screw access, controller/carrier clearance and normal/training connector access. None of these positions, cutouts, holes or insulators has been implemented in native CAD; there is no released mechanical assembly.

The source interface remains a local conduction bottleneck. No filled/capped vias, premium laminate, custom pockets, fan or plastic-top thermal route is proposed. Source joint inspection/X-ray, flatness, grease thickness, clamp pressure, bracket tolerance and tab isolation must be specified and measured for the later assembly. A common plate alone proves neither thermal nor electrical safety.

Outdoor construction needs a **ventilated raised guard with at least 15 mm vegetation/soil stand-off**, unobstructed entry/exit paths and shade. Support the guard independently; fins must not rest on dry leaves/grass. Upright, horizontal and sideways/restricted conditions are sensitivities, not qualified installation ratings. An enclosed or leaf-obstructed cooler receives no favorable catalogue credit. A 10 W solar sensitivity makes even the retained cooler unacceptable without shutdown. An accessible-guard target ≤50°C and stop criterion 55°C remain proposed conservative field-use constraints, separate from device limits and requiring measurements. They are not ignition thresholds or a fire-safety certification.

This is a plausible simpler assembly than E5, but it does not close [local PA protection](M5B_E6R1_PA_THERMAL_SAFETY.md). The selected next decision is thermal/sensor closure, not another heatsink search.
