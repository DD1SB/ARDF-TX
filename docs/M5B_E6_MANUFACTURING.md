# M5B-E6 manufacturing disposition — HOLD

**No M5B-E6 manufacturing candidate was created. Do not order E6 boards from these analysis files.** The [review decision](M5B_E6_REVIEW_DECISION.md) stops before native layout changes under the mission's Phase D HOLD provision. E5 manufacturing is temporarily HOLD pending the thermal/mechanical correction, as directed. Its historical archives remain untouched; no controller or RF80 disposition is changed.

The active native [RF2M project](../hardware/2m/RF2M.kicad_pro), board, schematic hierarchy, electrical BOM and footprints still describe E5. Relabeling/exporting them as E6 would falsely imply that the common-cooler placement, insulation and cutouts had been implemented. Therefore no E6 Gerbers, PTH/NPTH drills, stencil, five-board fabrication ZIP, native 3D/exploded assembly or released machining drawings are produced. Their absence is intentional and explicit, not a successful reproduction result. The concept PDFs are clearly labeled simplified feasibility drawings.

The future E6 manufacturing constraints remain: 150 × 110 mm preferred outline, 140 × 100 mm mounting pattern, two-layer standard FR4, 1.6 mm, 1 oz, green mask, white legend, lead-free HASL, ordinary plated through vias, no special stackup/impedance service, filled/capped/plugged or premium thermal vias. Local routed openings are allowed only after real cutout-to-copper, washer and tool-access checks. Do not modify RF/source geometry merely to obtain a thermal number without RF review.

Fresh unchanged-E5 [native DFM](M5B_E6_RF_REGRESSION.md) passes standard geometry but retains 0.15 mm via-annulus local margins. Neither those margins nor the 11 native courtyard warnings were concealed. An E6 zero-warning CAD result is not claimed. Standard CAM acceptance, solder joint yield, HASL flatness, drill registration, clamping and source-joint X-ray remain supplier/process/first-article tasks.

[Review package](../hardware/2m/review/M5B-E6/README.md) contains power/thermal source code references, calculation tables, curves, interface/cost/concept comparisons, fresh baseline check logs and SHA-256 inventory. [Reproduction instructions](../hardware/2m/review/M5B-E6/REPRODUCE.md) keep old reports immutable and distinguish analysis completion from engineering acceptance.

After analytical/source-path closure and independent review, the remaining work is native common-plane placement/support/cutout implementation, full mechanical assembly and insulation audit, zero-warning ERC/DRC/parity/unrouted gates, all populations/safety/RF regressions, fresh native manufacturing/assembly exports and independent artifact review. Physical Gate H is not required merely to obtain controlled bare evaluation PCBs, but it remains mandatory before operational transmitter release. No order, public-main push, acceptance tag or manufacturing release was made.
