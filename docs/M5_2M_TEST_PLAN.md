# M5A staged 2 m evaluation gates

Date: **2026-10-15**. **All physical tests NOT_RUN.** This is a future laboratory procedure after separate approval, not authorization to assemble, energize or transmit. Use the actual RF2M schematic/BOM variants and record instruments, calibration, reference planes, ambient/case temperature, supply limits and raw data. Initial work needs one source/driver/PA assembly, not6–20 transmitters.

## Shared equipment and reference planes

Current-limited3.3 V and adjustable6–17 V laboratory supplies; energy-limited pulse/fault fixture; calibrated RF generator plus a qualified≥+25 dBm temporary driver for standalone PA tests; VNA with DC blocks/bias-safe fixtures; spectrum analyzer covering at least1 GHz; calibrated mean/PEP or envelope measurement;10 W or greater50 Ω dummy load;40 dB protection attenuation with the first section rated for full continuous input; oscilloscope suitable for144 MHz waveforms (approximately500 MHz preferred), differential/low-inductance probes, RF envelope detector, DMM/current logging and independent thermocouples. A low-bandwidth scope/envelope detector can measure AM but cannot qualify the RF gate/drain waveform.

Do not connect the normal PA directly to an analyzer. At5.14 W PEP,40 dB attenuation leaves approximately−2.9 dBm; verify the actual chain's power, peak and DC ratings. De-embed pads, detector, cables and filter loss. Training uses its own calibratedµW-sensitive50 Ω plane; avoid analyzer noise-floor claims without a calibrated source/substitution method.

At least three independently assembled RF samples must eventually demonstrate repeatability; one tuned sample does not qualify a6–20-unit fleet.

## Measurements simulation cannot replace

Actual144 MHz large-signal gain/stability/matching; driver compression/hot current; RF-envelope depth/THD/AM-to-PM/PEP; off-slot and cross-port leakage; national unwanted emissions; supply fault timing/overshoot and asynchronous reset races; hot SOA/thermal contact; antenna ERP/pattern; real discharge energy and12/24 h runtime. Every resulting report must retain raw data and a NOT_RUN/FAIL/PASS disposition, rather than changing a calculation into a measurement.
