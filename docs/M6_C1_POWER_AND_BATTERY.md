# M6-C1 — power and battery contract

The controller accepts a protected external2S/4S pack, approximately6.0–16.8V, on keyed J1: **1 PACK_RAW,2 GND,3 passive pack ID**. No charging circuit is fitted. J1 is the controller branch, not a PA distribution connector.

F1 is a Bourns SF-1206F100-2 fast1A/63V fuse (50A interrupt rating). Its0.132Ω typical resistance has±25% tolerance at25°C. The initially drafted1206L100/24YR code could not be verified and is rejected; no6V-rated fuse is permitted here. D1(STPS2H100A) blocks reversed battery voltage. The18V-standoff SMBJ18A is after D1; its cathode is package pad1. D4 is a low-leakage S1M silicon rectifier from PACK_PROT into VIN_SYS. USB supplies VIN_SYS separately through TPS2553 and low-drop STPS1L30A D3. BZT52H-B5V1 D21 bounds reverse leakage into the TPS2553 output; an otherwise floating output could slowly exceed its6.5V operating range. The calculated15mA/100°C leakage scenario predicts5.89V using the Zener differential resistance and temperature coefficient. This is an engineering extrapolation requiring a bounded power-only hot-leakage test, not an unrestricted fault guarantee. GPIO37 reads inverted VBUS presence through Q2, and the USB PHY must detach when VBUS disappears. A10k VBUS bleeder discharges leakage. D4 prevents USB from energizing the pack sense/presence node; a10k bleeder bounds hot reverse leakage. A Schottky here would create an avoidable false-pack-presence risk. No reverse-current/charging path from USB to a battery is intended.

AP63203WU-7 provides fixed3.3V,2A, with3.8–32V recommended input and35V DC absolute maximum (40V only for400ms). Input capacitors are50V rated; the4.7µH inductor is a shielded Bourns SRP5030T-4R7M. At16V input, calculated ripple is about0.51A. The output bank is2×22µF plus MCU/local capacitors. Nominal capacitance is not its biased effective value: bias curves, stability and load-step measurements remain open. No raw4S signal reaches a low-voltage device.

The TVS's stated clamp voltage is a datasheet pulse condition, not protection against an unlimited wrong supply. Defined qualification inputs are bounded laboratory6–16.8V, with a separate current-limited transient/fault fixture. Do not inject unqualified faults from Li-ion packs. Short-circuit behavior, fuse coordination, diode heating, ripple, ADC errors and shutdown ramps require measurement.

The selected TLV803EA30DBZR uses the **standard DBZ map1GND/2RESET/3VDD**. Code30 means3.08V, not3.0V; A denotes200ms reset delay. MCU_EN also directly gates RF permissions. This protects reset sequencing; it is not a precision RF-rail compliance detector. Scope the actual power decay and module fail-off before operation, including the interval below the nominal RF3.135V supply range.

## Pack identity and compatibility

The harness carries10k to ground for2S or22k for4S, fitted at the protected pack-side controller branch. Host10k pull-up gives nominal1.65/2.269V. Open, short, unknown code or contradictory measured voltage refuses RF. The resistor is a wiring/configuration identity, not an authentication device or substitute for checking the actual battery.

Battery ADC measures **PACK_PROT after D1**, divided470k/47k. Its nominal ratio is1/11;16.8V gives1.527V and the29.2V TVS pulse reference gives2.655V. At16.8V the raw battery is above the sensed point by the load/temperature-dependent diode drop. Treat inferred raw voltage as an interval, including approximately0.1–0.79V diode-drop uncertainty and divider/ADC errors. Do not claim a calibrated fuel gauge. Apply conservative undervoltage guards, validate them with source measurements and record the resulting accessible battery energy. The hardware3.3V rail can operate at6V input even where software/local RF protection already inhibits transmission.

Pack changes, an unknown assembly record or an invalid ADC sample age withdraw ARM/mute release. USB-only configuration is supported with all RF logic power outputs inhibited by the independent pack-present gate.

## Harness and current

Use external fused star distribution from **one common identified protected pack**. Independent simultaneous2S/4S packs are unsupported by this single-ID design. Both interfaces may be installed when their assembly populations match the same pack: RF80 full plus RF2M BUCK4S on4S, or RF80 Fox-only plus RF2M BOOST2S on2S. No simultaneous full-power operation is required. Keep separate positive/return pairs to each RF board J301, with no PA current in IDC cables or controller PCB. An initial reproducible arrangement is distinct2S XT30 /4S XT60 pack adapters, labeled module-population branches, JST-VH mating housings/contacts for RF inputs, and the separate3-pin XH controller branch carrying its passive ID. Use genuine rated parts; these harness positions remain unquoted.

For PA branches use18AWG copper, initially≤0.5m one-way. Approximately21mΩ round-trip conductor resistance gives84mV/0.34W at4A, excluding contacts. Branch fuses must be coordinated with the actual RF population, wire and protected pack; start with a bounded bench source, not an assumed5A unrestricted pack test. Controller22AWG wiring and1A protection are adequate for its reserved logic load. Preserve all three RF80 and all three RF2M IDC ground returns. The controller's separate logic grounds do not carry the intended PA return current.

With AP63203 reference tolerance ±1.5%, the calculated regulator range is 3.2505–3.3495 V. The final native copper contact-graph model charges every complete traversed segment at 80°C, 35µm copper and 10µm via-barrel sensitivity, with no credit for parallel planes. The final `connector-rail-budget.json` includes those actual copper paths at a conservative0.40 A upstream load and150 mA port load, **0.088Ω hot TPS22918 resistance**,0.080Ω harness allowance and5 mV ground-reference allowance. Both calculated connector minima must exceed3.135 V; exact numbers are generated from the final board. Scope and load-test this budget rather than treating the model as measured compliance. Only one RF logic bank is normally powered; initialize ports in sequence. WiFi/BLE remain off during normal ARDF operation; optional LoRa TX occurs off-slot until immunity is qualified.

Controller-only awake budget40mA at3.3V is0.132W; each reserved150mA RF logic port adds0.495W and LoRa120mA TX adds0.396W. These are allocations/estimates, not measured consumption. Add converter/diode loss and the pack-presence bleeder. The12h maximum/24h reduced targets require integrating the complete transmitter's measured transmit/idle power with2S2P/3P/4P or4S1P/2P usable energy, temperature, aging and cutoff. Continuous beacon/Fox roles cannot borrow the20% Classic duty factor. M6 does not close those existing endurance gates.

Normal controller steady-load design budget is0.40A at3.3V, with only the selected RF logic bank powered during operation and WiFi/BLE off. The0.92A total allocation is a short transient reserve, not a continuous thermal rating. The manufacturer's89K/W buck figure uses a2oz fixture; a150K/W1oz sensitivity with80% efficiency predicts89.5°C junction at40°C/0.40A. Continuous0.92A would predict154°C and is prohibited by this model. Actual regulator/diode temperatures, capacitance under bias and burst response remain physical gates. LoRa TX is scheduled off-slot with a single powered RF branch.

The IDC ground returns are signal/reference returns; the dedicated heavy harness is the intended PA return. Shared grounds can carry some parallel return current, so do not claim mathematically zero PA-related current in a ribbon cable. Keep the heavy RF returns short and joined near the modules, never substitute an IDC return for J301 ground, and measure cable/ground offsets during RF commissioning. The3-pin XH connector is internal to the identified adapter harness and is **not** a2S balance-connector interface; do not expose it as an interchangeable raw-pack socket. Verify the keyed external XT30/XT60 adapter and every conductor before connection.


USBLC6-2SC6 pin5 is the positive ESD bias rail and is connected to local3.3V/C10, not raw connector VBUS. Otherwise a self-powered D+ pull-up could charge an absent VBUS through its steering diode and falsify VBUS detection. Its manufacturer VCC application permits the3.3V bias; scope the actual attach/detach and ESD behavior. USB-only power intentionally powers the controller but cannot grant RF permissions.


The native route report and `incremental-endurance.json` provide a repeatable budget rather than importing obsolete M2 RF-supply losses. Using M2’s unqualified 11.9 Wh/cell ×0.80 aging ×0.90 window ×0.90 temperature provision gives 15.42 Wh for 2S1P, 30.84 Wh for 2S2P/4S1P, 46.27 Wh for 2S3P and 61.69 Wh for 2S4P/4S2P. A 40 mA awake host at an assumed 85% conversion efficiency uses 0.155 W; the reserved 150 mA RF logic port adds up to 0.582 W. RF PA, diode/bleeder, cable/BMS and optional-radio loads must also fit the 12 h /24 h average-input budgets. Continuous Fox/beacon operation cannot use Classic’s 20% transmission factor. These planning factors do not establish accessible pack energy or qualified endurance.

The final port switches are **TPS22918DBVR**, replacing the draft TPS22919 whose0.115Ω allowance was not a hot maximum. TI specifies80 mΩ maximum at2.5/3.3 V over−40..105°C and88 mΩ at1.8 V; the model uses the larger88 mΩ envelope for the3.25–3.35 V interval. The DBV SOT-23-6 landing is larger than DCK and was implemented with local rerouting, not a generic footprint relabel. Physical map is1VIN/2GND/3ON/4CT/5QOD/6OUT. CT is intentionally open as permitted by the datasheet; QOD is tied to OUT. Mandatory external ON pull-downs retain default-off. This switch has no internal current limiter: buck cycle-current limiting, the controller fuse and supervisor reset removing595 output/POWER permission provide the fault response. Bounded output-short and ramp tests remain NOT_RUN. No RF module load switch was changed. [TI primary datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/gpn/tps22918).
