# RF2M — M5B-E5 compact engineering candidate

The active native design is 150 × 110 mm with the RF80-compatible 140 × 100 mm mounting pattern. It retains standard JLCPCB two-layer 1.6 mm / 1 oz FR4 and all E4R1 circuitry, source-joint geometry and passive cooling. Controller and RF80 hardware are unchanged.

Read the [review decision](../../docs/M5B_E5_REVIEW_DECISION.md), [compact layout](../../docs/M5B_E5_COMPACT_LAYOUT.md), [thermal/mechanical review](../../docs/M5B_E5_THERMAL_MECHANICAL.md) and [review package](review/M5B-E5/README.md). E5 is a candidate for independent review, with no acceptance tag. All physical qualification is NOT_RUN; the conservative 110°C thermal target remains unmet and the 125°C gate remains OPEN.

The archived [E4R1 package](review/M5B-E4R1/README.md) is the immutable 180 × 140 mm fallback. The last accepted CAD tag remains m5b-e3. New E5 fabrication data must never replace an older archive. No upload, procurement, assembly, energizing or RF operation is authorized.
