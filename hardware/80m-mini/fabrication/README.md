# M80-E1 fabrication review package

**Engineering GO for five bare evaluation boards only. No order has been submitted.** The package is independent of the existing three-family manufacturing plan.

Use `Mini80-M80-E1-JLCPCB-bare.zip` for CAM review: 35 × 35 mm single board, two copper layers, ordinary FR4, 1.6 mm, 1 oz finished copper, green mask, white legend. ENIG is preferred for the pogo/fixture pads; no gold fingers, castellation, blind/buried vias, controlled impedance or special RF laminate. Use the actual Edge.Cuts path, not its 0.05 mm plotting stroke, for the 35 mm outline. Separate PTH and NPTH drills are in mm; both share absolute coordinates with the Gerbers. Two NPTH mounting holes are 2.2 mm. Routed signal vias are at least 0.45/0.20 mm; stitching vias also use 0.60/0.30 mm. No countersinking or slots.

`Mini80-job.gbrjob` records the layer mapping. Gerber file functions identify front/back copper, masks and silkscreen; `.gm1` is the closed board profile. Drill maps and `drill-report.txt` are review aids. Copper/edge clearances, short/open circuits, courtyards, footprint parity and solder-mask checks passed native KiCad DRC. Local geometric DFM checks verify 0.10 mm minimum track width, 0.20 mm via drill, 0.125 mm actual minimum via annulus and 0.25 mm minimum SMD copper-pad dimension. A project rule enforces at least 0.15 mm between different-net copper pads, and the mask minimum web is configured to 0.10 mm. These checks use [published JLC capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities/); they are not a manufacturer CAM approval.

The ATtiny1616 local footprint widens all twenty peripheral lands to 0.25 mm at 0.40 mm pitch, leaving nominal 0.15 mm adjacent pad gaps. This includes the corner leads; no hidden narrow custom anchors are relied on. The 1.7 mm exposed pad has four paste windows. U2 and U3 each have one ordinary small ground via in the exposed pad; its native via settings request tenting. The exposed-pad mask opening exposes the top hole, so only bottom tenting can be expected from the resulting mask artwork. There is no paid resin-filled/capped via-in-pad process. Inspect solder wicking/voiding and processor/boost joint wetting on first assembly; stencil/reflow is required. These low-power parts do not depend on high thermal dissipation through the via.

`Mini80-M80-E1-stencil.zip` contains F.Paste, empty B.Paste and the outline. The supplied stencil is generated from a disposable copy with DNP L30–34/C30 removed, matching both bench populations. The full native board retains the optional paste geometry; regenerate a population-specific stencil if the antenna population changes, removing its mutually exclusive zero-ohm bypasses. Use a 0.10 mm stainless stencil and normal fine-pitch paste/reflow process; confirm paste release at the 0.4 mm QFN and 0.5 mm MSOP. This is a PCB/stencil package, not an accepted JLC assembly order. J1, PPTC and crystals should follow their manufacturer reflow profiles.

The BOMs are under `../assembly/`. The native KiCad position CSV uses absolute mm coordinates with Y in the KiCad export convention; its rotations are native KiCad rotations, not yet verified JLC rotations. The companion assembly PDF shows pin-1 markers. A derived JLC-style centroid CSV is provided for review, but machine-library rotation and exact supplied part orientation require assembly-vendor confirmation before PCBA. Do not auto-submit a placement file without that review. Bare-board CAM is independent of this assembly HOLD.

All passives are top-side. Bottom pads are the pogo interface, not components. Fit the exact MINI50 or FOX10 ballast population; do not mix values between the eight outputs. Battery/antenna wires and strain relief are fitted after reflow. Antenna loading coils and their parallel zero-ohm bypasses are mutually exclusive. No panel or global manufacturing history has been changed. Export reproduction: `/usr/bin/python3 ../tools/export.py` from this folder, followed by `/usr/bin/python3 ../tools/verify.py`.
