# Connector, local copper and mechanical closure

RF80 J101 and controller M6-L1 J4 are the same keyed Würth 61201621621 family, with the manufacturer 61201623021 female IDC counterpart. The [pin matrix](controller-compatibility.json) verifies every native and exported pin; no controller update is made. Looking at RF80's component top, pin1 is native(27,55)mm, pin2(29.54,55); odd pins run down +Y in 2.54mm increments. Both component-top and carrier-top views are unmirrored. A female cable face can appear mirrored when viewed from the mating side: key/red stripe alone is insufficient. Ohm-test each conductor 1→1 through16→16 before connecting supplies.

RF80 nominal supply is3.3V (3.135–3.465V, connector absolute3.6V). Controller outputs use Ioff buffers and100Ω series resistors,10k host pulls and approximately47k local pulls. FAULT_N is open drain and intentionally low while disarmed; nominal sink about1.5mA with the controller pull network. The high level around2.7V is assessed in the retained controller contract, not redefined as5V logic. LEASE software-progress edges nominal50ms; ARM low/MUTE_N low while retuning; at least1.5ms quiet before deliberate rearm. KEY high at rearm or contradictory paths/profiles is rejected. PACK4_OK derives from actual pack validation, never a fixed high. See [binding controller contract](../../../../../docs/M6_C1_RF_INTERFACE.md) and [safety](../../../../../docs/M6_C1_SAFETY_AND_FAULTS.md).

The controller is the sole3.3V source. Its per-branch switched supply, partial-power-down/Ioff buffers, unpowered-high-Z I²C repeater and analog isolation remain unchanged. A powered PA pack with an unpowered controller must not supply logic through the ribbon; topology is retained and physical leakage/sequencing checks remainNOT_RUN. Do not connect a second external3.3V source to a connected controller header or hot-plug an armed cable. Dedicated J301 carries fused pack current1VBAT/2GND, not J101. Full normal build needs validated4S; FOX_ONLY omits the normal converter/driver/PA. Its2S support is mandatory; any additional pack configuration must satisfy existing sensed-pack/voltage restrictions. The short silk “FOX:2S” identifies its inexpensive minimum configuration, not an approval for arbitrary battery voltage.

The 29 changed hole sizes are in [finished-hole-audit.json](finished-hole-audit.json). A lead diagonal calculation leaves radial allowances half the quoted diametric values. Manufacturer lead-position/pitch and PCB registration can consume those margins; it does not prove all multi-pin corner combinations. Incoming measured lead limits and natural no-force dry-fit of the complete array are explicit first-article checks. No connector substitution, forced lead spreading or precision-hole service is hidden in this design.

All tracks/vias, component placement and pad centres are exactly preserved. Only J502 outer width grows0.2mm. Its re-filled plane clearances change materially only X148–157/Y120–124mm, with sub0.000001mm² integer-polygon roundoff elsewhere. No normal RF path or transformer route moved. Bigger drills reduce annular copper, but nominal revised PTH annuli remain≥0.25mm. For a pessimistic short local0.10mm-wide/35µm-thick copper strip,1mm length gives about4.9mΩ at20°C, around11mW at1.5A before temperature correction. This local estimate does not qualify a fault current or replace the actual high-current/supply/thermal measurements; the external fuse and driver-isolation gates remain.

Mounts: four3.2mm NPTH at module-relative(5,5),(145,5),(145,105),(5,105), unchanged140×100mm pattern. Keep insulating supports clear of both sides; initial underside lead/service clearance≥10mm, coil/power envelope≤25mm aboveboard subject to actual windings/heatsink, cable service allowance30mm with≥20mm bend escape. [Dimensioned outline](mechanical-interface.svg), actual KiCad top/bottom/angled renders and [native audit](mechanical-fit-review.json) are supplied. Local WRL winding/header/BD140 models are only envelopes, not supplier-certified fit.

IRF610 tab is drain; BD140 collector is live. Neither may contact a grounded sink/support/cable shield without a suitable insulator and shoulder bush. TH301 remains remote and insulated against the PA case with strain relief; moving it to cool PCB ground is not equivalent. Inspect thermal interfaces and measure hot SOA. Flexible side-by-side harnessing avoids assuming RF80 and controller mounting holes coincide. Keep RF2M's25.2mm underside heatsink/airflow envelope free. No final enclosure or certified heatsink fit is claimed.
