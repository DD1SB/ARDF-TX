# R1 controlled controller commissioning — NOT_RUN

This is one controller and one staged procedure. It is not authorization to build or energize hardware. Keep RF modules/raw RF packs disconnected, LoRa DNP and the backup cell removed until instructed. Use bounded laboratory supplies; never start fault testing with Li-ion packs.

1. **Unpowered inspection.** Confirm the selected base population and every exact MPN. Inspect D22 Nexperia BAS116 SOT23 pin1A/pin3K/pin2NC and D23 Nexperia BAT54H SOD123F pin1K/pin2A. Do not substitute onsemi's differently packaged BAT54H. Check R3=27k, R8=180k, R9=33k, R19=100k and C25=100n. Verify J1 pin1PACK_RAW,2GND,3ID and the unchanged IDC pin1/key orientation. Inspect both sides against assembly views and pad audit. Trim connector leads and retain at least10mm underside clearance. Use nylon supports; avoid exposed conductive mounting/contact under the pack circuitry.
2. **Fit the protection network first.** Leave U10/U21 unpopulated for the initial fault test. Fit the passive pack-ID network, input protection/regulator components and their specified decoupling. Clean flux residues and dry the board. Six small underside positions can be locally reflowed with controlled preheat before final THT assembly; follow paste/component limits, avoid overheating the RTC/module and inspect the opposite side after reflow. No special via filling or factory assembly is required.
3. **Bounded power/ID tests.** Current-limit the main controller supply and independently current-limit the ID-fault source. Inspect rails with a DMM and oscilloscope before increasing the limit. At8.4V and16.8V main input, test10k/22k IDs, open and GND. Apply defined+8.4/+16.8V ID faults relative to board GND and measure U21.5: expect the documented divider range, below2.74V with U21 absent (including the leakage sensitivity; resistor-only initial1% corner is2.6472V). Measure supply/diode currents and no rise of V3V3 through the ID path. The ID source need not supply high current; use a few mA maximum and verify actual resistor dissipation.
4. **Negative/transition qualification before MCU fitting.** Test−16.8V ID with U21/U10 still absent, including intended temperature extremes. With the main battery input absent, also measure PACK_PROT/D2 and BAT_DIV: ID excitation can draw the otherwise unpowered protected pack node negative through R3/D22. Establish safe limits at both ADC inputs before fitting U21/U10. Verify the PACK_ID clamp remains above−0.29V (and ESP absolute lower limit−0.3V), rather than assuming the25°C pulsed diode specification proves continuous/cold behavior. Abort on an unsafe voltage. Then fit U21, leave U10 absent, and use a bounded3.3V supply/ramp to measure both U21 sides and the ADC pad during startup, shutdown, brownout, abrupt supply loss and ID hotplug. The±0.204V off-state calculation applies to VDD=0; measure isolation timing during ramps. Do not expose U10 to an unqualified transient. Correct any failing waveform before continuing.
5. **MCU/USB-only logic.** Fit U10 only after supply/analog conditions pass. SW1=RESET, SW2=BOOT, SW3=START, SW4=STOP/hold reset, as defined by the native nets. Use recovery/USB configuration with RF raw power disconnected. Verify all RF enables default inactive during reset, bootloader, USB attach/removal and brownout. USB-only operation must leave PACK_PRESENT inactive and RF banks inhibited. Check3.3V and VBUS leakage under battery-only/USB-only/both, including hot reverse-leak cases from M6-C1. Do not assume the buck sinks ESD current. An IEC test is a separate controlled test, not improvised contact with a charged conductor.
6. **Qualify the ID reader.** Select frontend revision2, PACK_ID ATTEN1/2.5dB and the930mV acceptance ceiling. Reject ADC saturation before voltage decoding. Check the actual attenuation/calibration configuration; other ADC channels use their own ranges. Measure the actual ADC transfer and both reference resistor codes across pack and operating-temperature limits. Confirm source drop/leakage/error bounds; the example calibration record is not measured data. Reject missing, ambiguous, stale, wrong-version or out-of-envelope calibration. Wait≥40ms after stable power/ID change and require repeated consistent samples. Confirm open/short/positive-fault/incorrect-code observations never authorize INIT_OK/PACK4_SOFT. Verify fresh invalid samples withdraw INIT_OK/ARM and latch a fault. Use the reported full unqualified envelope to identify where tighter measured bounds are necessary; never force the nearest code.
7. **USB/RTC/interfaces.** Confirm full-speed enumeration, pull-up detach when GPIO37 reports absent VBUS, clock setting and backup behavior. Configure RTC backup explicitly; no primary-cell charging. Check the two isolated I²C branches independently with modules absent/unpowered. Use continuity-tested keyed cables, module-specific preparation/arming protocols and no shared addressing bypass. Measure loaded3.3V/ground offsets at the IDC connectors before connecting a powered RF module.
8. **Fail-off and RF integration.** After controller-only gates pass, retain the original module-specific protocols. Test loss of real application progress, reset, status faults, stuck buses, wrong population/pack and conflicting band requests. No automatic rearm. Only later authorized RF integration may establish off-slot silence, real A2A, PEP, emissions, temperature and endurance. Those RF80/RF2M qualifications remain independent.

Record actual voltages, currents, temperature, scope traces, ADC calibration/version, USB events, RTC drift and fault timing against serial number. Failure leaves Gate D OPEN. No physical result in this procedure has been performed by the CAD milestone.
