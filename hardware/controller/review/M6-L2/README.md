Each `BOM-<RF>__<LoRa>__<option>.csv` and corresponding placement CSV is a specific population. BASE/GPIO retain recovery R300/R301; FOXPRO/BOTH replace them with R302/R303. The open service links are never fitted in standard builds. A no-LoRa/no-FoxPro/no-GPIO build remains valid. FoxPro battery voltage is unavailable for both 2S and 4S; it is not a calibrated voltage-reading mode.

The manufacturer STEP is retrieved into a local reference cache for rendering, not redistributed as MIT material. Existing connector/RTC dimension-only envelopes retain their qualifications. Conditional spacing in the mechanical review is an installation constraint, not measured fit or RF coexistence evidence. Physical qualification of assembled controllers, genuine FoxPro2, antennas, accessories, protection faults, power sequencing and RF/thermal integration is Gate G and remains NOT_RUN.
